Addition to rules: fakenicking

2005-10-25 19:47 by ?ke Vader

As we've seen several clans having fakenickers on their screenshots, and are fed up with this phenomenon, we decided to do something about it.


Addition to the rules:

Fakenicking is not allowed, even if the fakenicking player is listed on the clans roster but under a different nick. A player has to play with the nick he's listed as on the teams roster or else the clan will receive a warning. After two warnings the team receives a punishment where 1 point is deducted from their stats.


The reason for this is that it adds a bit of hazzle to the confirmation process and it confuses other players who follows the QW action as well. From now on we will issue a warning to the team for every violation of the rules, and after two warnings one point will be deducted from the teams stats. If anything is unclear, then please say so in the comments.

About the EID vs DoS incident yesterday we decided that we won't punish them for using their two players who weren't on the roster. We will issue them a warning though. The reason for this is that EID played the game after all when they could've checked their roster before they decided to go on with the game.
