Playoffs/Qualifying

2005-11-16 14:41 by term

I've updated the rules regarding playoffs and qualifying. We do realize that many of the non Div1 clans want playoff as well but you have to wait for EQL2 :/. We are not satisfied with some of the divisions and our goal is to start EQL2 asap after EQL1, hopefully with more even divs after the qualifying matches.

Playoff/Qualifying:

Div1 top 4 teams - semifinals and final or 3rd place match.
Div2-5 top 2 teams - 2 qualifying matches to step up one div.
Div1-4 bottom 2 teams - 2 qualifying matches to stay in current div.
Qualifying groups - the 2 teams from the same Div keep their points from their previous battle.
Only the final is best out of 5 maps, all other matches are best out of 3 maps.
