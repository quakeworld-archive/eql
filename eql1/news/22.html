Playoffs & Qualifiers commencing

2005-12-07 22:56 by ?ke Vader

For the clans who are about to play qualifying games and playoff it's now time to do that. You are expected to do this during this week. As you can see in the premiership playoff division table, it's Fragomatic vs Suddendeath and Firingsquad vs Antiquad that are to be played (nr1 vs nr4, nr2 vs nr3). Never mind the division table here, we'll take no consideration to it, it's playoff style that matters. The winners will face eachother in a grand final later on.

In the other divisions it's qualifying time. The only matter to make clear here is that the teams from the same divisions brings the points from their previous game they've had this season. That only makes you have to play two matches versus the clans in the other division. You're free to go on with both qualifying games and playoff games whenever you want to, i know some clans have already started. The sooner they're played, the better. :)
