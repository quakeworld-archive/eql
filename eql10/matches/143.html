#5 2009-11-03 09:52 by Hooraytio (85.228.255.XXX)

This debate (misplaced clans) is as old as the division system.

1. there will always be a winner of each division
2. that said winner might have been misplaced
3. there will always be a clan ending last
4. that clan might have been misplaced

This doesnt change the fact that there would have been another winner and loser of that division if those 2 clans where removed. Is a clan misplaced just because it starts out the season with 2 wins? Snba havent even played vs CN yet which is another strong clan in div4.

When saying that snba wouldnt survive in div3, by that you mean that the clan would lose too many games and quit?

Each season the admins must make a decision about all the clans and ever since the division system was introduced there has been some major or minor error resulting in many or a handful quite boring games.

The solution to this could be to have a preseason ladder with all the participating teams to decide their skill lvl but ofc this suggestion has some flaws as well:

1. Time: The ladder would steal time from the real season making it hard to end the season before x-mas/summer depending on time of year.

2. Consistency: The qw scene is in a constant state of change and far too many clans are very short lived making it hard for the admins to keep track. Also during/after the ladder som clans would vanish and new would be formed. Would the new clans be allowed in the season without playing the ladder?

3. Recruiting: A new player can heavily alter a clans skill level and make it hard to place it accordingly even if it did participate in the ladder.

4. Faking: A clan could fake their skill level during ladder play to end up in a lower division.

As it is now the admins try to determine each clans skill level and place them accordingly and i think it is good enough even tho some clans slip into the wrong division.

Other solutions?
