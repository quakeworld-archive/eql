bored, trying to find out how to write a report for the game but can't seem to find it so I'll add it as a comment instead.

Game started out with some classic pingwhine. Not the kinda pingwhine I'm used to seeing in eql but we thought it had gone missing for some time so we decided to start the game SR-style with some psychological prewar and we suceeded with the pings in typical swedish fashion.

CMT3: Not much to say, we were in the game for around 7 minutes but at some point they got too many weapons and we never really got back in the game. Lack of routine on the map decided this one.

E1M2: Nice pick from us, the russians are a fine team on this map so I was a little sceptic at first however nabbe and riker made sure we could win this pretty comfortable. Felt pretty safe from the start to finish although they got a few rls and control shifted at times.

DM2: Now this map was a thriller. The start was a complete nightmare. They got a nasty lock on us from the first minute and the scores ran away like crazy. Think it was 5-100 after the first 5 minutes and the russians had a player with over 40 frags by the 7 minute mark. However we had a few rls here and there but they pressured us really well and we took too long to gain map control. We never really got a complete lock over them and control shifted a lot through out the last minutes and they could just slow spawn to safety. WP by the russians.
