About EQL 11!

2010-02-24 00:18 by 1tsinen

Hello fellow quakers! A new season of EQL is up (signups) and we have tried to keep the working parts of last season with minor tweaks, so not many surprises to come. For this season we want the players to take more responsibility for coverage and...

... similar stuff. We will open a EQL channel on the mumble voice server (voice.quakeworld.nu) for people who wants to try commentating games (mainly div2-4 depending on the amount of divs ofc). Maybe we can find a new skilled commentator this way? Well this is ment to be kept at an amateur level and will be there for fun. The division 1 games (atleast the crucial ones) will be provided with streams and commentators (pro/semipro) if YOU provide the info when the games will be played a couple of days before or so.

Other coverage: Well we hope to see many previews during the start of the season by the players them selves. We will also (admins) provide a preview or two but we would also like to see 5-10 previews that we can publish on the qw.nu/eql-pages. Reviews of the situation in midseason are also welcome, so you don't need to ask us permission to do these things. We will gladly take your help and efforts to make the scene better and more dramatic (in a good way). Or if somebody wants to do an interview or similar stuff, you are more than welcome!

Some slight tuning of the rules which had some minor unlcear parts which have been fixed. One new Ezquake beta version allowed so you can use "the best route"-feature.

Well we are hoping for a very nice season and happy fragging! Btw, Ownage, EQL, Clanwarz.... QW is ALIVE!
