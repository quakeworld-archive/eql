#2 Elguapo - 2010-04-14 10:49 (130.243.188.XXX)
Some reporting then:

E1M2: As usual quite ffa'ish with a lot of twists and turns where no team ever had total control. We had at times decent control with 2 RL's at YA/GL area and could thereby snatch a few quads. Our quadrunning was complicated by the sneaky russian tactics and we struggled with getting a good fraging going. However, we had the slight advantage during the main parts of the map and so also ended out on top. ggs.

DM2: Not much to say here, russians and dm2 is like a happy marriage and we never had much of a chance. This was made easier for them having a very good start with good control after just 2-3 minutes. We had to struggle at low-ra/mega and gather RL's for some not so successful attempts of reconquering Quad. Well played, CCCP.

DM3: Decider coming up, and we felt had a fair chance here. Very tight game indeed, where the Creature stood for a very solid performance whereas my own contribution was rather pathetic. Was just like 10-20 frags dividing the teams up until 2-3 minutes left when CCCP sealed the deal with some winning Qruns. Could have gone either way tbh. GGs.
