#14 Purity - 2010-03-30 18:41 (94.212.9.XXX)
It's hard to say that e1m2 doesn't work on a high level, it's just a completely different map. At times the outcome of games can rely on a single dropped RL pack if the one that picks it up manages to go on a streak that dimman was talking about. Even though it's less common it does happen at times that there are minute long RL battles around ya/gl or quad side for control with 20 dropped packs which make no difference whatsoever because the ones picking them up are as good as dead already.

Even so I've seen teams retreat to mega for minutes because the other team has one stacked player running ya+gl+quad with scores remaining fairly even because the defense around mega and ga+rl side is good enough to keep the quadrunning team from scoring more frags or getting more weapons to ya+gl safely.

You can always end up in a game where you don't have a RL for longer than seconds so you end up with just sg, ssg and sng but what I really like about that on e1m2 is that you can make a serious difference with those weapons because of the lack of armor and rockets.
Joined efforts in SG spamming a stacked player can be very rewarding just like managing to keep enemies from taking RL even if you die yourself seconds later.

Obviously all those reasons can be turned around and be given as why people hate e1m2, it's just a preference.
