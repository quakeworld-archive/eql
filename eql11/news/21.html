EQLadder

2010-05-26 21:14 by 1tsinen

Yes, you did read it rights.. action coming up soon!

EQLadder info:

During the summer EQL will host a 4on4 ladder tournament for all interested clans in Europe. Other clans are welcome as well but the games will be played on European servers. And some quick facts and rules:
Matches will be best of 3 and one of the TB3 (dm2, dm3, e1m2) will always decide the outcome if the score is 1-1 after 2 maps.
If 2 of the TB3 maps have been played the remaining one will be decider.
If 2 custom maps have been played the clans will throw one map each. Random select will tell which clan throws first.
If 1 TB3 and 1 custom map have been played then random select will decide which of the 2 remaining TB3 maps will be decider.
The teams can play as many or as few games they like during the season.

Teams.
A team can hold any number of players as long as no player is registered for several teams.
A player cannot join a new team unless his old team has gone inactive, in which case he cant rejoin his old team if it is revived.
A player currently not in any team can join any team he likes.
New teams can join the ladder at any point during the season.

Versions and cheating.
Latest versions of ezquake and fodquake are allowed.
No cheating. This includes but is not limited to hacking, ghosting, dropping on purpose etc etc.
Denying to play a certain map is not allowed, if you dont like a certain map then dont agree to play in the first place. If you can't get an answer from these rules to you problems then read the EQL11 rules first and see if there is a solution, lots will be the same.

Versions and cheating.
Latest versions of ezquake and fuhquake are allowed.
No cheating. This includes but is not limited to hacking, ghosting, dropping on purpose etc etc.
Denying to play a certain map is not allowed, if you dont like a certain map then dont agree to play in the first place.

Playoffs.
The ladder will go on between 2 set dates and then playoffs will begin (typically 2 weeks before eql12 starts, so final will be during first week of eql12, nice transition)
When the season ends the top 8 teams will go into playoffs where bo5 applies to all games.
TB3 will decide playoff games as well but the clans can choose from the pool during the first 4 maps. Random select chooses which clan to start)

The maplist has atleast the following maps:
TB3
CMT1b-3-4
Schloss
Grim
e1m5, e1m6, e2m2, e2m7, e3m1, e3m2, e3m3, e3m6, e3m7

We will listen a short while to map suggestions. There are also some maps that most probably will be included that wasn't listed!
