2010-09-23 21:38 by 1tsinen

The final divisions are up and we hope you will start playing as soon as possible.

This season will require something like 1.5 games/week so there isn't too much time for slacking. We do also hope you will advertise your own games with the qwbot (serveme) even if you are playing in div1, 2 or 3. There are always people up for speccing some games and this usually helps people know when it's EQL games and when it's just a prac.

So far there has been very slim contact considering previews about the divisions. We hope to get as many previews up on the pages as possible but as many people as possible. This usually gets some great speculations going.
