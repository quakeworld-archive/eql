New beta client of ezQuake allowed

2010-10-04 23:28 by

The majority of servers today is running with antilag turned on. In short antilag allows you to shaft like if you had 12ms no matter your actual ping. It also affects sg etc (article about it can be found at quakeworld.nu).

Problem has been the visual representation of the lg beam when playing with high ping.

Read on for more information and client update!

Before antilag was implemented, having 51ms and shafting was horrible. The LG beam was laggy and delayed due to the high ping. Antilag changed this server wise, giving everyone a 12ms shaft beam in theory.

Problem with the visual model of the beam on high ping still remained. There was just no way to know where your shaft actually hit when on high ping.

ezQuake devs implemented cl_fakeshaft 2 (to be run with cl_fakeshaft_extra_updates 1 to work) to draw a visual shaft beam equal to playing with 12ms and cl_fakeshaft 0, no matter your actual ping.

So in other words, this client update is mostly for high pingers (but applies to everyone with 12+ms) to be able to get an accurate view of where their shaft is actually hitting.

Commands again:
cl_fakeshaft_extra_updates 1
cl_fakeshaft 2

This represents a 12ms shaft beam with cl_fakeshaft 0 no matter your own ping (which is what antilag is giving you).

This client will _only_ be allowed until a stable release is coming.

You can find win32 (gl+soft) binaries here.

(Thanks JohnNy_cz!)
