#42 HangTime - 2010-09-21 18:29 (77.98.152.XXX)
Good things about big divs:
-More games for each clan
-Usually more games that the weaker clans in the division can be competitive in (i.e. the ‘not-really-div1’ clans in div1 can have several decent games against each other rather than only getting smacked down hard by div0, i.e. they are grouped together rather than split across div1 and div2). This is one reason why TKS were happy to play div1 last season because we felt there were at least 4-5 clans we could have close games with. With a smaller division it would have been more daunting. Obviously, you will always have some clans on the boundary.
-Less chance of clans getting in playoffs ‘by default’
-Forces top clans to maintain activity to avoid getting overtaken by highly active average clans
-Widens spectrum of clans for prac/ladder games i.e. more clans who can meet “.qw prac now div2?” criteria (or in other words it reduces the ease with which clans can decline pracs based on the division of their prospective opponent). Being low div1 sucks when div1 is small because hardly anyone will play you.
-Reduced risk of one or two inactive clans screwing up the division

Bad things about big divs:
-Bigger blowouts (tVS vs 15th best clan = GG)
-‘Midtable mediocrity’ syndrome can set in towards the end of the season whereby clans end up having no chance of playoffs and also no chance of being relegated, so have little to play for.
-Fewer top level playoff games (i.e. 3 divs = 3 finals, 4 divs = 4 finals etc)
-Potentially more daunting for people doing coverage… (lol?)
