And I'm getting seriously tired of Paras pointing fingers at us, Fallen Angels due to one of our players.

No matter what kind of team sport, a team always contains many different skilled individuals. It's how you combine these individual skills into a team effort that define your teams skill.
To raise your teams skill level, all individuals need to raise their skill from a team perspective. Yes, you need to raise your personal skill level as well. But a team with lesser skilled individuals who know how to act in as a team will always be stronger than a set of higher skilled individuals who doesn't know how to play together.

No matter what division your team is in, you will always have different level _and_ type of skill in your lineup of individuals. But it's the performance AS A TEAM that define which division you're best suited to play in.

I can beat and/or give a good challenge to div1 players when it comes to aim (DMM4). I can also beat some of them in duels. Because I can do that, am I a div1 4on4 player? No.
Place me in a div1 4on4 team and I will look like a complete newbie (like I do in div3 sometimes as well...). All that says about me is that I have some parts of the skill sets needed to be a decent 4on4 player, but that I lack other parts.

Anupko is an overall slightly better player than the rest of Fallen Angels, but to no degree is he a whole division above the rest of us in his general 4on4 teamplay skill set.
As a teamplayer, anupko's skills may cover some of the flaws of us other members. But if we stopped supporting each others flaws, anupko and the rest of us would be raped every match.
