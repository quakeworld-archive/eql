Walkovers

2010-11-18 11:34 by Hooraytio

The season has ended but all games have not been played. What happens now one might wonder?

Since there are a few unplayed games we need to get information from the clans as to the reason for this.

Please contact an admin on irc (#eql.qw) or by private message on the qw.nu forum if you have a walkover request or if you have other information or questions.

If you do want to get a walkover you will need to present information, like irclogs or screenshots from a conversation ingame, that show that you, during the season, actively tried to get the game played.

In cases where both clans on different occasions tried to scheduele the game, but for various reasons it never got played, we can have a situation were the points will be splitted between the two clans.

Contact us as soon as possible as we are aiming at having the playoff fixtures up by Sunday the 21st of November.
