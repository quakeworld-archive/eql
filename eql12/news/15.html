Playoffs!

2010-11-21 23:18 by Hooraytio

The group stage is over and the playoffs are upon us! Read on for further information and quarter final fixtures.

We have seen more activity than in a long while and especially division 1 has been great at playing their games. Exalted with Mcdonalds have had their games cancelled but the activity points for their opponents will remain. It is actually fair that teams who were active and tried to play the whole season get to keep these points. Remember, clans that started to play after 4-5 weeks are really at the mercy of the admins and we still let those clans stay in the league.

Division 1 quarter finals:
Slackers versus Cccp
the Viper Squad versus QuakeKlan
Fusion versus TeamKillers
Suddendeath versus Sauna

There is not much to say about the walkovers in division 2 as they did not really affect the standings radically.

Division 2 quarter finals:
Bad Luck Troopers vs Demolition Crew 2
Chosen versus Oclan
Satanic Slaughter Clan versus Magnum 44
In a Blaze versus Cross Defenders

In division 3 there are no surprises either.

Division 3 quarter finals:
Psy versus Southern Smoke
Machinery versus Mob of Oddballs
Rusty vs Challengequake Proworld
Paras versus Fallen Angels

Deadlines are:
Sunday 28th of November for the quarter finals.
Sunday 5th of December for the semi finals.
Sunday the 12th of December for the finals.

Remember that playoff matches are played best of five and that no map can be played more than once.

Remember to inform the scene about what day and time you plan to play your games!

Good Luck!
