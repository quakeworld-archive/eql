EQL:Pro

2010-12-06 02:27 by 1tsinen

As quite many know there will be a small league..

.. consisting of only the best players and clans out there. Top4 teams will be invited while the other 4 teams will have to qualify for the last spots. All teams are welcome to join up and the qualifiers will be played on sunday the 19th of december starting at 18:00 cet.

We also hope that the bronze game considering division 1 will be played, since we might use seedings on putting teams into groups.

The actual EQL:Pro league will be played in january on sundays, with strict schedules which are NOT negotiable.

Players from the teams which did not qualify also has the chance to join up with the teams that qualified. This is also recommended for the clans that made it to try to scout out the top players to make sure they have players in their roster. If a computer crashes or a player goes missing when the games are supposed to be played it can't be helped.

For the players: Check out if your team is going to join the qualifiers in time. If not then joinup another team. This is surely something your don't want to miss if your a high skilled player.
