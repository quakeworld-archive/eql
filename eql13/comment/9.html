Less than one week left!

2011-03-30 23:14 by 1tsinen

As many have noticed we are getting closer to the end. The activity...

.. has been quite good. There is still room for teams in playoffs atleast in division 3. Lots of them have a couple of games less played than the ones that seem to be getting quite certainly to playoffs and could throw us a thriller or two.

Division 1 has been both good and bad. The 2 games system seems to have worked for several ones, but then there still seems to be quite many clans beeing "semi active" which causes problems. Atleast with deciding on WOs and similar stuff.

We should not forget to mention division 2 which seems to have played almost all their games. I hope the last ones will see the daylight within the few last days. Here I would guess we will not see too many dramatic changes anymore this season.

As most have noticed we are getting closer to the summer all the time. People start doing other stuff than play quake (wtf?). So I believe we will manage without a catchup week this time.

Also REMEMBER the following rule considering the playoffs: Teams in Division 1 must report 2 days ahead in semifinals and further when they will play their games. Also Division 2 & 3 has to do the same considering the finals.

Also we would be intrested in knowing if there are any active players out there that would be intrested in helping out us admins in the fall!
