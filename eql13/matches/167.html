#5 gaz - 2011-02-24 01:07 (90.202.42.XXX)
DM3:
Wow, what a thriller! We had the marginally better start and had control of RA for the first 5-10 mins or so, but we failed to take quads and make them really count, so our lead was never very much. Paras managed to take pents 3 and 4 and built a small lead of about 20. We took control back with about 3 minutes to go and with a minute left we had a deficit of around 10 frags. Paras went into hide-mode and we went into seek-mode. We scrambled a few frags in the last 10 seconds but it didn't look to be enough, until a Paras player strafed onto one of my pines in the RL box with exactly 1 second left to tie the scores. I checked the demo and this was really something - the Paras guy is in no danger of being hit but then he strafes a long way to the right and the pine goes off under his feet with the countdown on "1 second". Of course, we had some kit and were able to take the overtime pent and the first few quads, and had good map control for all of overtime.

E1M2:
Paras had a good start and didn't let up. They got a lead of about 30 but then we pulled ourselves together and we snuck into a lead of 1 or 2 frags. It didn't last long though and Paras held the lead for the rest of the game. We did a nice job at YA but Paras took lots of RLs and always used them well at the quad room, thus stopping us from dealing out big damage.

DM2:
Another thriller! I think we had a slightly better start and we started to build a small lead - it was around 50 frags at one point. Then with about 7 minutes to go, Paras took over and as we frantically flooded to try and get it back, our lead dropped right down to around 20 and for a few moments, even less. I don't know who took it back but Pattah made a couple of really important streaks with RL and with quad, and Stefan and Manny did a good job in the last 2 mins to seal the win.

Overall this was a nice first competitive game for us since November (?) and our first win in EQL since last spring. We haven't practiced much and it showed at times on all 3 maps - hopefully we will improve because we have stiffer challenges ahead.
