Quake Summer!

2011-04-14 14:30 by Hooraytio

What should we play during the summer, one might wonder? EQL13 is nearing its end and EQL14 is still quite a few months into the future.

Have no fear, NQR summer ladder is here!

Gaz has been working hard to restore the NQR season archive. He also dug up the old NQR Ladder site and decided it was time for a comeback!

At the moment you and your team can sign up for two different ladders, 2on2 and 4on4. The map pools will be large and each game is bo3 but decider maps will be tb5 for 2on2 and tb3 for 4on4 unless the teams decide upon a solution of their own. Gaz has said that there will be a 1on1 ladder as well so keep your eyes peeled on this site

Happy summer fragging!

*edit* 1on1 ladder is now live! *edit*
