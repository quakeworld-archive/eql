Division structure

2011-09-16 11:37 by Hooraytio

There has been a lenghty discussion about how many divisions EQL14 should have. Well, here is your answer.

EQL14 will consist of 3 divisions. There are too many teams, yay!, to divide them into only 2 divisions since the season might drag out too long when 15 teams try to play vs eachother.

There are also too few teams, in our opinion, to divide the them into 4 or more divisions since the threat of idle clans would have a major effect on the number of games played in a certain division. This also applies even if the teams meet twice since every idle clan takes away 2 games from each opponent. Also, it is first and foremost a competition so you will have to count on that there are some teams that will be very hard to win against while other teams probably will be kinda easy, unless you are in the rock bottom. We will, however, correct any _serious_ cases of misplaced clans as we see fit.

We know that there are players with different, well based and soundly argued opinions. However, this is the path we have decided to follow this season as well.
