League system

2012-02-28 14:57 by Hooraytio

This is how things will work this season.

The 23 teams will be divided into two (2) equally skilled groups according to a seeding made by the EQL admins. This means that we are not using 3 or more divisions based on skill and/or previous known clan performance like we have in previous season. The reason for this has been put out there in previous news posts.

The groups will be called A and B in order to make it easier for the clans to distinguish between the two groups. Group A will hold 11 teams and group B will hold 12 teams. Should a team sign up late it will be added to group A. Should more teams sign up late they will be evenly distributed between the two groups.

The regular season will run for 7 weeks, starting on wednesday the 29th of february and end on sunday the 8th of april. During this time each clan shall play versus the other clans in their group once.

There will be three (3) different playoffs based on the final standings in each group.

The winner of each group will go directly to the Gold tier semifinals where they will meet the winner of the quartefinal between the runner up from the other group and the 3rd placed clan from their own group.

Clans ending on places 4 through 7 will enter the Silver tier playoffs where they will meet a clan from the other group in the quarterfinals. 4vs7 and 5vs6 respectively.

Clans ending on places 8 through 11 will enter the Bronze tier playoffs where they will meet a clan from the other group in the quarterfinals. 8vs11 and 9vs10 respectively.

As of now this leaves the last clan, 12th placed, of group B outside of playoffs. Should all clans stay active we are leaning towards having a qualification game between clan 11 and 12 of group B so that every clan get a post-season game.

All regular season games are to be played best out of three (BO3) and all playoff games are to be played best out of five (BO5).

The groups will be up later tonight and you should be able to start playing no later than tomorrow.

Happy fragging!
