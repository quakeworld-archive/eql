#10 Hooraytio - 2012-03-28 10:09 (85.228.252.XXX)
Even dm3 was originally constructed as a duel map :P. In the beginning of 4on4 even dm5 and dm1 was played alot along with all kinds of obscure episode maps. I guess after a while it boiled down to a few "playable" maps as the skills and teamplay developed. Maps like dm5, e3m7 and e1m6 just dont work very well when players get more skilled. It is too easy to camp key areas and win.

I think dm2 is popular still because it got the "2 bases" structure and it is very clear what area your team has and what area the other team has. The concept of the map is easy and it can be executed by all clans as long as they play equal opponents. In low skill matches dm3 and e1m2 tends to be more messy and it shows that the players are more uncertain about what to do.

On very large maps it isnt as clear where to go and what to do in order to find and kill the enemy team. On dm2 you either attack water to try and take quad/tele or you attack low to try and take rls and take out rl enemies in order to avoid counterattacks on quad/tele :)
