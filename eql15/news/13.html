Final weeks of groupgames

2012-04-04 12:07 by Hooraytio

We are glad to see that more clans have started playing. Therefore the groupstage has been extended one week. You have until April 15th to play your games. Read on for more info about that and the playoffs.

We have extended the groupstage to the 15th of April instead of the 8th. You will need to play any remaining games before then or claim WO versus clans that have failed to show up or refused to play versus your clan.

Clans that lost 1 or more games, all 0-2, and then stopped playing will be giving WO to the rest of the active clans. Clans with 0 games will have all their games voided.

You will only need to apply for WO vs clans that have both won and lost games but failed to show up for your game or even refused to play you. Irclogs or screenshots from qw proving your case is recommended in this situation.

WOs between clans were the situation is even more delicate, like wanting to play but didnt find the time will be handled from case to case or even voided if no clan has a better reason for a WO win than the other. We hope these games can be resolved before the 15th of April so we dont have to deal with WOs and VOIDS between active clans.

The clan with more points will not have the right of way in all situations. Even a clan that lost all their other games can be given a WO against your clan if you are the ones who didnt show up for the game.

We will not remove any clans so even the ones with few or 0 games will get to play in the quarter finals if they wish. If not, they are idling or have quit alltogether, their opponent will be given a WO and go through to the semifinals. The bottom clan from each group will miss the playoffs unless they can replace some clan from bronze playoff quarter finals that isnt active anymore. Most likely the bottom clan isnt active either but if it could make a quarter final happen instead of WO its just much better.

All quarter finals must be played before April 22nd. Keep an eye on this page for your upcoming quarter final opponent. In Gold playoffs the top team from each group will reach the semi finals directly and therefore will not have to play during the first week of playoffs.

GLHF!
