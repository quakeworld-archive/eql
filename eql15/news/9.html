Warming up

2012-03-05 09:53 by Hooraytio

Lots of pracs and 4 more EQL15 games.

The 4on4 scene feels really vibrant at the moment. Ever since EQL15 signups opened we have seen an increase in activity and especially during week 9 the 4on4 matches peaked with 188 maps played. The clans also seem to find prac partners outside of their former comfort zones wich was also one of the goals with the system we implemented for this season. A hope of bringing the scene closer together.

SSC is the most active clan so far this season with 2 wins and 1 loss in group B. They didnt have any problems versus TPA and Mini-cute while Slackers proved to be too hard this time. In the other group we saw Chosen taking a fairly easy 2-0 victory versus Smackthatass.

So far we have seen games that should have been pretty easy to predict the outcome of and I agree, they have been one-sided indeed. But try to bare with us this season and give it a shot before you call us various names of the not-so-kind variety.

Prac versus the clans you deem fitting for your current level and see the much harder official games as a chance to try out what you picked up during pracs. I know that most of you out there want to have fun and compete but competing and fun doesnt hold the same meaning to everyone. Thats why you probably wont see all the games as your version of fun. Its just too bad that we dont have enough clans to make it super fun and ultra competetive with 7 divisions were the tables final positions will be seen in the last game of the season.

Also, I think it is highly unlikely that a clan in either group will lose all 11 games, not to mention getting raped in all 11 games like I have been told by some will happen to their clan. Funny enough, several clans told me the same thing so that makes it bordering the impossible ;). Try to look forward to the playoffs and do your best this time. Give us feedback during and after the season but try to keep it on a civilized level. This is by no means the final solution but with the current situation it was the best thing to do.
