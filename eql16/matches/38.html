#5 fix - 2013-05-06 10:18 (80.220.32.XXX)
Quakeworld is full of wonders. It struck me as a big surprise to notice Oclan's performance vs tVS at this very site before our planned encounter, yet another was hidden in the screenshots when the fact was inspected further. There he was, the mighty Rikoll, supported by bunch of rare puritans of the New World.

Expectations for 3points had flown out of the window. Well a pity activity point would do, as we are way behind the schedule and in dire need for points.

As Mr.Kuntu already pointed out our game is nowhere near people commonly would think, people with little more depth insights and knowledge might see it only fitting considering involved players recent years activity and picked up routines.

I personally think 4on4 gameplay has evolved much in terms of "speed and dynamic overall flow" in last 5years.

Things happen even faster than before and it seems almost impossible to hook up a sync with correct rhythm to chaos surrounding you. Might be explained with lack of routines too, but i think natural evolution supports the idea.

I would like to think i had good/fast movement skills back in the days but if you could have a race between me from 2005 and Milton from current day, the difference would be clear as a day.

In the golden years of CMF we really didnt have any certain tactics, we had a common game philosophy and 4 good overall players filling in the duties as needed in each situation. Of coz there were patterns in roles from time to time, but that was comfort of being in the situation to be able to choose a quad runner etc, basic idea was just to do what was necessary.

It worked almost to perfection sometimes, winning nqr7 without voice when it had already become somewhat a standard in div1 4on4 speaks for it loudly in my book.

The problem is that atleast i still try to play by the same gamebook, its just that lack of routines in all aspects leaves me naked and wounded to the wolfs.

Time to get back to the basics boys? ill be the tele guy!

Enough of the mouth running. Oclan firmly showed us how to get things done.

On e1m2 we managed to hold our end surprisingly nicely, i guess pretty one dimensioned map structure played a part here, ya/quad ftw.

On dm3 when they finally got the control. frags started rolling in freakishly ratio. mad skills.

dm2...well with better start it might have got bit more interesting, on the side it felt like our psyche had been hammered already too long at this duration mark.

Well played oclan, it was fun and good game, with all the highs and lows.

When I signed up with Oclan, this match, together with the match vs tVS, was definitively the games I was looking forward to in the group stages.

To be honest, this one didn't feel like my best game. But where I failed my teammates really stepped up and got me up on my feet again shortly after, and I could continue what I feel I do best in this game called QW: constantly pressure and be as much of a pain in the ass to my opponents as my abilities allow me to. (well, maybe not exclusively to QW after all)

I really felt like I was being carried, and not the other way around in this game, which makes me really proud to be a part of this team. Even though I'm always confident of winning, I am also very proud of this result looking at the strong lineup we faced here. I also feel that we as a team just keeps playing better and better at a pace I've never experienced anywhere else before.

Hopefully our performance in the group stages is enough to bring us to the playoffs, and I believe any team we might possibly face there will have to bring their A-game to the table to stand a chance. We're going for gold.
