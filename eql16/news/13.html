End of season and walkovers.

2013-05-08 12:00 by Hooraytio

As the season is drawing to an end I have some info about walkovers.

Season ends on May 12th and we will accept games that are reported even during the night between 12th and 13th.

If your clan has had problems with getting other clans to play you then you can report a walkover and contact Hooraytio with information as to why you report a walkover.

You dont need to wait until Sunday to report so you can start now if you have any games to report as wo's. Should the other clan be able to play during the last days it is up to you if you want to try and get it played instead.

Clans with very few games and only 0-2 losses will most likely be giving walkover to the active clans they have not played against. Games between them will be voided. Other situations will be viewed individually for the best outcome.

/Hooraytio

PS

This is how the playoffs work:

Division 1 will have 11 clans and the top 4 clans will go directly to semifinals in the A playoffs while the next 4 clans will go directly to semifinals in the B playoffs. We will see 1vs4 and 2vs3 in the A playoffs and 5vs8 and 6vs7 in the B playoffs. All group games are best of 3 and all playoff games are best of 5.

Division 2 will have 9 clans and the top 8 clans will meet in the quarterfinals where we will see 1vs8, 2vs7, 3vs6 and 4vs5. Winner of 1vs8 will meet the winner of 4vs5 and winner of 2vs7 will meet the winner of 3vs6 in the semifinals. All group games will be best of 3 and all playoff games will be best of 5.
