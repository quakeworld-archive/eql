Signups

2013-08-09 10:38 by Hooraytio

Some info about signups here!

Signups are open from now until September 15th so go ahead and sign up and get some warmup games going.

As you can see I have added a warmup division for EQL 17 where all signed clans can play versus each other and report the results just like a normal EQL game. This is supposed to be for fun and also to get some action going. Your clan is not required to play any warmup games but I guess it wont hurt to get some pracs in before the real group games begin.

For starters I will add fixtures for all signed teams and you can contact me if you wish to play more than once versus certain clans.
