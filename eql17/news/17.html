After one game late on sunday and a few walkovers and voided games the correct table is:

1. Immortals
2. Fraggers United
3. Chosen
4. Klaphat
5. God Blessed
6. Paras
7. More Bastards
8. Lost Legion

9. Division99
10. The Team

A. Immortals vs Lost Legion
B. Fraggers United vs More Bastards: 20 Nov 21 cet
C. Chosen vs Paras
D. Klaphat vs God Blessed

All games are best of five (5). The deadline for these games is November 24th.

The winners from each semifinal will meet in the final and the losers from each semifinal will meet in the bronze game. These games are also best of five (5). The deadline for these games is December 8th.
