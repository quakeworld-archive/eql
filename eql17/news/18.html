Division 1 playoffs

2013-11-18 22:30 by Hooraytio

After a very tight season we have reached the semifinals in division 1. Here is how it all will go down.

Having seen tVS take down SD 2-1 in the final game of the division 1 group stage we are left with the following matchups:

The Viper Squad vs The Norse Nov 24th 20cet
Suddendeath vs Firing Malfunction Nov 25th 20 cet

The games are best of five (5) and the deadline for these games is November 24th

The winners from each semifinal will meet in the final and the losers from each semifinal will meet in the bronze game. These games are also best of five (5). The deadline for these games is December 1st.

Best of luck and cya on the servers.

/Hooraytio
