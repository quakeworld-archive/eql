#7 ovr - 2014-04-16 13:28 (212.126.160.XXX)
I wish I could say we did well this game, but honestly, that would be a lie.

I was expecting a high level performance, almost taking the match to the tie brake like we did last time (http://eql.quakeworld.nu/eql18/matches.php?id=39#Comments), but it seems that the Quake gods (not Milton) were against us this time.

Even with my team putting full effort to conquer one more victory, SD was very well trained and effective, having the control of the game the whole match. We even tried to turn the things up, like we did on the last game which was decided only on the last minute, but this time there were no magic that could help us. It was a rapefest and the few frags we managed to make came only due some telefrags.

I didn't only lost, but felt intimated by that score.

Nothing more to say, I would like to congratulate the whole SD team that did great. Also all the other teams, players and Hooratyo who once again managed to make a nice league for our fun.

In special, say thanks to all QTV users who were supporting us, especially to Sane, who is spreading my knowledge and skills around, based on that topic: http://www.quakeworld.nu/forum/topic/6359/97113/great-opport unity-for-naeu-communit#97113

Greetings to all, good luck on the finals and see you all next season.

ovr!
