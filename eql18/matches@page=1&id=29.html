#51 Azamat - 2014-04-07 17:22 (162.150.162.XXX)
Look, I'm no carebear. Open PvP is the most imatnoprt feature for me in any sandbox game. However, making safe zones out of the major cities doesn't bother me in the slightest in fact I think it's a good idea for exactly the same reason AV does. It's necessary to make this small compromise to attract a significantly greater portion of players. I think there's a significant number of players who are lukewarm or hesitant towards 100% open PvP, and this gives them the opportunity to gain a greater appreciation for open PvP without diving in head first. Most people don't start swimming in the deep end. They still have to leave the cities to do almost anything anyway.Even if it takes away more or less from the open PvP feel (less for me), it's worth it for me to play a game that will have a stronger player base and survive. I think in this case the compromise is worth it to attract more players. The MMO market isn't going to become sandbox overnight it's going to take at least some compromise during a transition.
