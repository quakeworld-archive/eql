League structure

2014-09-29 10:38 by Hooraytio

Since the total amount of clans is uncertain at this point I thought I might share my thoughts on the structure of this season.

It is my belief that as long as we have people who want to play Quakeworld 4on4 there should be some sort of league to cater to their wishes to do so.

There was talk about bringing back Salvation but since nothing happened I decided to give EQL another go. I would have been perfectly happy playing Salvation as well since it is a good way to remedy the lack of clans and to bring all the active players together.

As of now there are 5 clans signed for EQL and if this doesnt change before signups close on Sunday I am thinking of using a system where all clans play eachother twice followed by playoffs for the top 4 clans. This can easily be modified to include more clans if lets say 8 or 10 clans have signed up before we start the season.

Bottom line is, there will be a season in some form as long as we have 5 clans or more.

From this day to the ending of the quakeworld. We in it shall be remembered.
We few, we happy few, we band of quakers.

/Hooraytio
