C playoffs

2014-11-04 08:32 by Hooraytio

As the season is progressing it is apparent that all the clans are active. This is unusual and I think this calls for some kind of reward for all clans at the end of the season.

I have decided to add C playoffs for the clans who end up on place 9 through 11. The C playoffs will use a round robin system to decide the winner.

This means that each clan meet the other two clans once and the clan with the most points will be the winner of C playoffs.

These games will be BO3 and will run during the same time period as the A and B playoffs. Most likely December 1st to December 14th, unless a catch up week is added to the regular season. In that case all playoffs will run from December 8th to December 21st.


Best of luck to all clans!

/Hooraytio
