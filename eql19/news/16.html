EQL20...

2015-08-10 10:42 by Hooraytio

...So I promised that EQL20 would be back in September 2015 since life prevented me from organizing it during the spring.

I am willing to keep my promise to arrange another EQL season and also sign up my own team, Chosen, hopefully gathering most of my teammates from last season.

Question is: Are there enough players around to form enough teams to make another season meningful?

The format would most likely be:
TB3
One division (two divisions if there are 16 or more clans)
All teams meet eachother once during group games
A, B, C playoffs so everyone get a shot at some playoff action
Season+playoffs end before Xmas as usual
All of this is subject to change if need be

What do you guys think?
