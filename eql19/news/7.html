Final standings and playoff fixtures

2014-12-01 10:22 by Hooraytio

The group stage is now over and the playoffs will begin this week. Have a look at the final table and playoff fixtures below.

The regular season has seen some good games even though we missed the grande finale, the game between Suddendeath and Fragomatic. They still have a chance to meet in the final or bronze game since they have qualified for different semifinals.

In other parts of the table I would like to give credit to Fraggers United and Division 99 for playing 9 games which was a nice feat in a somewhat limping table, even though Division 99's game versus Earwax is not counted in the final standings. Also Experience, Paras, Suddendeath 2 and Felia-020 deserves a shout out for playing 8 of their 10 games.


Final standings:

After a few walkovers this is how the final table looks like. Earwax's 2 games has been scrapped from the table since they stopped playing after 2 games.

Experience 23p
Suddendeath 21p
Felia-020 18p
Fragomatic 15p

Fraggers United 15p
Chosen 13p
Suddendeath 2 13p
Ping is no good 11p

Paras 11p
Division99 8p
Earwax 0p


Fragomatic reached A-playoffs because they won more maps than Fraggers United while both clans ended on 15p.

Chosen reached 6th place because they won more maps than Suddendeath 2 while both clans ended on 13p.

Ping is nog good reached 8th place because they won more maps than Paras while both clans ended on 11p.


A-playoff fixtures:

Semifinals:

Experience vs Fragomatic
Suddendeath vs Felia-020

All games are best of 5.
There will be a bronze game.
The deadline for the semifinals is December 7th.


B-playoff fixtures:

Fraggers United vs Ping is no good
Chosen vs Suddendeath 2

All games are best of 5.
There will be a bronze game.
The deadline for the semifinals is December 7th.


C-playoff fixtures:

Division 99 vs Paras

Since Earwax stopped playing the C-playoff winner will be decied with a best of 5 between Division 99 and Paras.
The deadline for this game is December 14th.


The deadline for the A and B playoff finals and bronze games is December 14th but I will come back to this once the semifinals have been played.

Best of luck to all teams!

/Hooraytio
