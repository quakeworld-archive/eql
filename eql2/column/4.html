Angua interview

2006-02-02 03:17

After calling Mr.Angua's agent for several months I finaly got an appointment for a interview and here it goes


Hello and thanks for agreeing to do this interview!

Hello my biggest fan


What is your irl status? I.e name (firtsname if you are shy), age, work/school, other basic stuff?

Adam Lindsj?, not shy obviously, 20... soon to be 21, hot stuff, doing the military service and gets out in 71 days


Give us a brief resum? of your qw career. How and when did you start and why do you keep playing?

began like one year after quake got released, some lanaction with my brother who was called daver, after a couple of years of sporadic lan-gaming I got internet
then me, daver, nikita and marx started a clan kinda
spartacus, we owned like every clan there was
then started playing cs, won eurocup and got inactive
returned to qw, joined X, played a season, joined bgsr, played a season there too, and now in cmf
btw. vladde and mrlame taught me alot in bgsr
and I play nowadays mostly because I still think it's fun.


What is the greatest moment in your qw career?

got a few, like winning a duel vs xavier, winning nqr, and winning vs fs every now and then, always gr8 fun


What do you think about EQL compaired to NQR and other tourneys?

I think it's quite the same to nqr, I like it, except for 3pointsystem... 2-1games should be rewarded as they are more fun to play, rapes suck


witch player do you look up to most in QW and why?

tough choice, but I think I'll have to say fifi, he - if anyone - is the most intelligent player ever (hint gaz)


What is your worst QW moment?

oh my, already at flamequestion? can't say _one_ moment, could start with bgsr-fs, mrlame gets banned fs gets wo in groupgames, or why not bgsr-fs playing 3on4 in semi/quarter...


Do you usually whine alot during games or do you belong to thequiet ones who just keep playing and have a fun time whatever the outcome?

a code we started in X was to never talk during the games... so no, I do not whine at all during the games, afterwards I can criticise, but no, not whine.


What is your comment on the milton pic?

hahaha


Who do you think is the most underated clan and player? and why?

hard to tell, overrating is easier... tho the angry harwester from aq is quite underrated
I'd say, clan I dunno, as I said, more overrating in div1.


Who is the most overrated player and clan? and why?

murdoc seems to be rated very high nowadays even tho he's quite the egocentric player, to not speak of the whining and eventual teamkilling. Clan, well there are a few... no need to put them up until the tournament is ove


Who do you think gonna win eql (if u count cMF out) and why?

LA will probably pull it off this year... they aren't scared of the opponents lineup, opposed to tVS for example


Give me a few comments of all players in cMF.. what you think about them etc..

siv - the man of big games, defensive player with nerves of steel. Our not so secret weapon for deciders.
jus(dare) - ra-camper deluxe, haven't played2much with him tho
fifi - like I said before, most intelligent player there is, offensive.
Zhin - same as dare here, haven't really played alot, but history tells here lies gr8 skills.
hlt - the helena, very offensive, and mostly jumps around like he has quad even tho he hasn't.
Vore - mostly plays defensive positions, aerowalk-guru.
Archi - Master of 2on2 together with me, ez win vs riker and rock ggthx.
vana - former sr member, more I don't know, have played like one map with him, where he was quite offensive.
mooniz - mostly speccing & playing wow nowadays even tho he's got madskills in dm2 bigroom teleports.
jester - the god.
fix - probably the perfect player, plays every position there is, and quadruns like only fix/fifi can.


We have heard that Carmack himself called you and said that he have renamed e1m2 to "castle of angua"! is this true? and from were does Castle of angua come rom?

It's actually true, probably because of cmf-gtg nqr semi where I topfragged over former e1m2-champs griffin & mrlame.


There has been alot of talk about this ilf dude from russia, that he cheated on an EQL game, some russians still say he did not cheat and just used low sens or something.. what do you think about this whole situation?

the russians who say he did not cheat are either dumb, brainwashed or just simply haven't checked the demos...


You have lost vs L0 and AQ in this EQL so far, how does it come that cMF (former NQR winners) loose vs previous eazy clans?

dedication quite obviously, we could've used a different lineup in those games, but we didn't as we want to give all our players who _want_ to play the chance.


What do you think about the community at the moment?

Let's see... last week I was in the army, tVS asking us for eql whole week, this week, when I'm home, fix and fifi available, they suddenly don't want to play. They say mirage is away for the whole week, but you see them prac with all 4 members daily. This, I'd say, is bad sportsmanship.


During the last years, we have seen a few topclans come and go. What ingridients do you think a team needs, to stay at the top and win games/leagues and do you have a good recepie to create a winning team?

Nowadays, the team is probably best with 4 allroundplayers, all who can quadrun and play defensive positions. Most clans use mm3 now too, something which kinda gives a different dimension to the whole teamplayaspect... we in cmf haven't bothered to get it, 2oldschool4that. But back to the question, further on it probably depends on the map too, as I like the setup of two defensive and two offensive players on e1m2 for example.


Some ppl like to play 1on1 some 2on2 some 4on4 and some pov/endif, but it seems that you love prewar, how come?

Probably because I'm the bestest there is on it... 1on1 is just hide, 2on2 can be fun if all players play 4fun and no hiding, pov/endif just suck.
and I have never lost a single prewar


Im planing to run a povdmm4 soon, UL won the last one vs Interceptor in the finals, the upcoming tourney will be held on wargamez without minping, who do you think gonna win?

I have no idea about povdmm4, probably the one with 12ms and a killer shaft, perhaps dave paradoks larsen ?


I have seen on some demos that you use tracklist during games to see who got pent/quad, and that you have tracklist bounded to mouse2, is that true?

actually, I don't use tracklist to see who got pent/quad, I use it to see if the specs are speccing me, which makes me win the tracklist, it kinda goes hand in hand with my prewardedication. If I get many specs tracking me, I'll do hot moves and everyone will be happy.
I do not have it bound to mouse2 tho, but y, like one button up from my moverightbutton


i know you always got the latest qw gear, the qw glove the hottest mouse and qw pants. What it the latest high tech qw item you have bought

there's this new qw-helmet.. it gives me +50% sg and 125hp when spawning.


What map is your favourite? and why?

e1m2, no shaft, many sg-duels, lots of survival and sneaky tactics are needed, perfect.


Rumor is that you played with ToT_Slabby in the topsecret clan gatugeng a few years ago...what skills and techniques did you learn from him?

I learned to not care about getting teamkilled constantly
and the topsecret take ring-tactic



Okey some fast questions now....

Sassa or Gaz ?
he he, gazsa

QW.nu or besmella?
qw.nu

Link or Kingpin?
HAHA, link

prewar or play EQL finals?
prewar, we're already out of reach for the finals :D

Fallbunny on or fallbunny off?
fallbunny off, more hot skillz needed

russia or poland
russia

Vladde or Manu?
gatugenget

Obv or SD?
as long as it's not the whiney SD's, i'll go for SD, as OBV are all whiney except muti and nas


Thnx alot mr.angua... some final comments before we call it the day?
sometimes, dead is better.



Thx to manu/vladde and nas for helping me with the questions
