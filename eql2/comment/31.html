Flame interview

2006-01-09 01:50 by sassa

Got some time with Flame from IRL in div4A


Hi and welcome,tell us abit about your QW history
I started play Quake in 1996. First year i played only on keyboard vs my friend Teether, which was the best quaker in the Czech Republic :). After 1 year i began play on the mouse. About 1997-1998 i did enter to my first clan RN (Radiant Nails), where i was for 3 years i think. After that i had age of Quake 3 ;) for 2 years in my second czech clan NR (The Necroraisers) in which still im but i dont playing quake 3.
Now Im in IRL (In Real Lifers).



Tell me abit about IRL, the clan you are in right now
IRL is clan about only 4 czech players which are the best in Czech Republic in QW i think. Four people which are Johnny_cz (student of High school), moris (student of High school) and me + shpeq (working ppl ;) )


What is your goals for this EQL2 Season?
We wants win 4a div sure:) ... I think we wants especially good play.


What do you think of EQL, how was season1 and what can we get better at doing?
Im not of one players which watching all about QW :\ and i think :) i didnt play season1 but EQL is from my side good league which has all:). Maybe i wish EQL with 2on2 and 1on1 with same style as EQL 4on4. ;)


Who is ur favorite QW player of all time and why?
Hm, difficult question ;) but my favourite QW player i think is Lakerman, coz i like dm2 and i liked him. He was which maked first time jump in dm2 from top over lava to ya-tele. :)


Tnx for the interview flamer, do you got anything else to say to your fans out there ? ;)
np and for fans i havent nothing... maybe some nice frags ;) and sry for my enhlish;)
