Div 2 getting active.

2006-01-09 13:45 by Horatio

Today i bring you the latest hot action from div2 with some of the top teams involved.

First out are Satanic Slaughter Clan vs Oblivion. This game, like sscs first one vs triada, also went to three maps with ssc winning dm3 again. However oblivion where too strong on e1m2 and cmt4. I didn?t expect ssc to win this game and it was strong of them to keep it together long enough too score a mapwin to get the one pointer. On the other half of the field oblivion showed that they are the team to beat this season with an all over strong game on dm2/cmt4, allthough they should be somewhat disappointed not to get a three pointer out if this game.

The second game featured two more clans from the expected upper half of the division as we saw Jokkmokk take on Hehe Holkeri H?visitk? Voittos?. The lack of prac and not having their best lineup made HHHV a quite easy target for the well trained and strong team of jmk as they where taken down 2-0 on dm3 and dm2. As expected their composition of 4 strong players without a clear starplayer made jmk a frightening force for HHHV.

In the third game oblivion made short work of el?intarha for another win this season. Only this time they didnt settle for two points. On e1m2 the mighty mutilator once again denied the oppnonents the win and on cmt3 it was a little closer but zoo don?t seem to have scared o too much on that map either. Thus ending the game 2-0 and getting three points to add to their previous two.

The fourth game was a bit of a surprise as fragunit took down triada 2-0 on dm2 and e1m2. Triada never got close enough to make it into a three mapper. Oho seems to have faired well in his move from div5 to div2 and made a descent amount of frags. On the other hand triada couldnt keep up the steam from their previous win and thus getting left behind with zero points after this game.

Finally we saw o once again in their match versus suddendeath2. These two teams are on my top3 for this division and i expected a close game betwen them. They didnt disappoint me at all when they started out on dm3 with a mere two frag win for sd2. This offcourse set the pace for the upcoming maps and the next one ended in a nice +40 frags for oblivion on e1m2.
Finally cmt3 was to be played. O didnt make many mistakes and ending also this map with a tightish +40 frag lead. Allthough sd2 did a nice performance i think they didnt have their strongest line for this game and i am now sure of my predictions of both oblivion and sd2 ending top3 for this season.

More news will be avaliable as the games passes by.
