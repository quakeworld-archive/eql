division 5

2006-01-10 01:17 by peppe

So, the last division, division 5. Lets take a look at the clans participating this seaseon shall we.
I would first like to say that division 5 has often been underestimated, but this season will be active and filled with action :)

To give a view of div5 first I will say that the most active clans will probably win this season, those being ccs, vkb, -oo- or sd.p
From t.g and JeDi I haven't seen a single prac or ladder, only reason why I don't believe in those teams. Misters a newly started clan with players like JSS and En3my could be dangerous.
J?garna has got a new recruit whom I've never seen before, who is this mysterious und? He might take them higher on the list.
shadow death and Loser Squadrun 2 I don't have so much faith in, shadow death I have only seen playing once and Loser Squadrun, sad to say, never.
Now division 5 can enjoy the sweet maps cmt3 and cmt4 aswell, hopefully this will lead to more unexpected wins and tight games.

Clan Cube Squad, I must say, this team is very active and might just take it all with nimfo in the lead.
These guys never give up and always say yes to pracs. They know cmt4 and cmt3 well so they have a big chance to win.
Strength: know all maps well, good tp.
Weakness: few
My tip: top 3

Suddendeath.project, the clan I'm in. Trying to get 4 players every day and searching pracs. We were very lucky to find good teamplayers
and at the same time having some time to prac before eql2.
Strength: all new players
Weakness: teamplay
My typ: middle section

Oldones, now these guys have been here a long time and they sure know where to stand. At the top of the list. I'd say their keyplayer is old, he knows how to
turn the map back and win at the very last minute. What I'm expecting to see from this team are nice dm3-games and lots of frags. Look out for Oldones.
Strength: players old and hoder, dm3
Weakness: cmt-maps
My tip: top 3

J?garna, as I said, who is this und fellow? I think J?garna can place themselves higher than last eql now that they are being more active and I heard they have taken on csn and won.
Now, not to be rude but these guys used to get wipped all matches, now I think they have seen the turning point and starting to play active, J?garna is a real threat to clans thinking to high of themselves.
Strength: players und and eggarf
Weakness: reputation
My tip: middle section

MisteRs, what to say? well I think these guys can play good and win some matches. Although they haven't played enough. They need more pracs, that or they already are mysterious winners right now :o
Even if they have good players, other clans know their tp better. I'm only speculating, this team might even crush the whole division.
Strength: players
Weakness: few games played
My tip: middle section

Loser Squadrun 2, led by link, oldschool player I'd say. I haven't seen this team play at all so I wouldn't be surprised to see them playing only 3-4 games this season. This team needs more devotion and more pracs.
Strength: Team leader link
Weakness: few games played
My tip: bottom section

shadow death, well, these guys has really gone far with their leet nicks. But to comment on their gameplay I have to say that they need more practice. All I've seen them do so far is run around on dm3 like crazy ants.
Still, I know very little about this team, but I don't dare give them too much hope since they have no familiar players on their line-up.
Strenght: willing to play
Weakness: gameplay and map knowledge
My tip: bottom section

The Gibbers, these guys played last eql but they didn't come high on the scoreboard. Sadly to say, clan-starter zerkki has disappeared from their lineup, the question how they will perfrom without him will be answered this season. Even when they took in div3 players they did not win too much. Doesn't seem to serious anymore in The Gibbers.
Strength: teamplay
Weakness: devotion
My tip: bottom section

Vitun Kova Bosse, now I know these guys have a big chance of winning. Their teamleader Jukka gives it all to win a game and he makes a good leader. When playing vkb I noticed they have a very good way of taking maps back in control. They never loose it
Strength: teamplay and players
Weakness: active members
My tip: top 3

SamoJedit, finnish clan Jedi always make me go: "What?!" They really know how to play, it's a shame I haven't seen them play anything since november last year. Maybe this will place them lower than what I first expected. Now that they lost their first eql-game they might start playing more(I really hope so). I don't know how well they know the cmt-maps but they will have to show it this season.
Strength: players and dm-maps
Weaknes: activity
My tip: middle section

This concludes my division 5 overview, if I don't get strangled I will return with more updates on division 5.
/peppe
