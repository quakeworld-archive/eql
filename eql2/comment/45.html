Div2 on the move. (wtf fakenicks)

2006-01-12 10:43 by Horatio

Yesterday another two games where played in division 2. Both games featured El?intarha. Unlike their previous loss to oblivion they really showed why they are expected to end up in the higher part of the division this time.

First out was the game versus osams wich started on cmt3. Tmoe made a nice performance here and they didnt let any of the opponents topfrag the map. Osams used their late addition harmacy who looked a little bit lost in cmt3 but i am sure he will find his way soon. They also used a player nicking lakso who is well known by most to be petter/xtalon, however the admin for the night (Term) didnt know this and warned the osams crew for using an unlisted player. (wtf fakenicks?)

Anyway, after the rough start on cmt3 osams faced continous problems on dm3. Once again Tmoe made alot of frags and keyplayer Gaz in osams faced some lagproblems like i have seen before the latest week. The map ended in a rather big win for zoo who walked out of the heat with three points.

Alltogether this game proved too hard for osams and i fear they will face some troublesome games this season eventhough i think they will hang in there and stay in div2 for eql3 also. Regarding zoo i think they will stay i the upper parts of the table and maybe even contest HHHV for their expected (my tip anyway) top3 position.

Zoo later this evening faced suddendeath2.

Zoo didnt take any chances and started out on cmt3 once again and also this time they where in the lead 1-0 after the first map.
Zoo are a team well known for their knowledge of cmt3/4 so this wasnt any bigger upset when the map ended and they had won it with 40 something frags even though phrenic in sd2 gave them some annoyance by topfragging the map.

Next was dm3 and this time a much closer call for zoo who apparantly where rescued by a telefrag on quadded sd2 guy by JKova late in the game and thus winning by a margin of a mere 6 frags.
The game then ended in a 2-0 win for zoo as and it showed that zoo had a nice selfconfidence from the earlier game vs osams. Nice three pointer once again for zoo and thus moving them in to second place in div2.

More news will follow, just keep the games coming all you div2 clans.
