Fragomatic calls it a day

2006-01-12 23:04 by ?ke Vader

After a very successful first season of European Quake League the EQL1 champions in Fragomatic has decided not to participate in EQL2. The reason for this is that they aren't planning to play as much as they think is required to compete in division 1 this season. This is some sad news as Division 1 would've been very interesting with Fragomatic competing amongst the old top clans like tVS.

However, we thank them very much for their Quakeworld propaganda games the previous season and wish them welcome back in the future. Concerning the individual players, they're of course welcome to play for other clans who wants to add them as "late additions".
