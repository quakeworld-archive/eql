Div2 keeping up the pace.

2006-01-16 15:13 by Horatio

I have the honor to present three more div2 games today.

First out was Oblivion vs Triada. This game was no surprise as o won it 2-0. However Triada came very close to make it a 3 mapper as o won by a mere 5 frag margin on second map dm2. If triada contiunue to play like that they will score some points in later games. Oblivion will stay on top and probably win div2 as things are now.

Second game of the evening was Loser Squadron vs Osams. Ls looks really strong despite having been inactive for a while and they took osams down in two close maps even though osams fielded a strong lineup for this game. Old star player tco also seems in shape as he topfragged both maps.

Third game this sunday was Loser Squadron vs Satanic Slaughter Clan. Here LS showed that they are back to stay as they once again won 2 maps in a row to score three points. SSC seems to miss milton quite badly and the other keyplayers couldnt really step up to the task in this game eventhough they came close on second map, e1m2. As things are looking now i dont think LS will be fighting as underdogs in the lower parts of div2 but rather challenge ssc for their place above average.

So far so good. A lot of activity and things are shaping up. We are still waiting for luis and death delares to play some games and some other clans have only played one game. Suddendeath2 have been somewhat of a disappointment so far but they have alot of games left so there is no need to panic just yet. Osams have scored two straight 0-2 games and need to shape up a little to stay in div2. Thats all for now, stay tuned for more reports as the games are getting played.
