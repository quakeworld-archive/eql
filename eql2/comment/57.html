Div 2 just keep playing.

2006-01-19 11:08 by Horatio

Today i have a massive amount of games from div2 to bring you.

D2 vs Zoo.
Zoo just keep on winning after their initial loss to oblivion. In this game the faced the poles in D2. They started out on cmt3 wich they have played alot lately and once again found themselves on top after 20min. This time without Tmoe JKova had to secure the top position and Gizmo made a strong debut. We also saw menthol playing for once. After this it was time for Polands national arena, dm2, but this time the poles didnt stand strong enough and the Zoo animals took a nice 2-0 victory.

SD2 vs Osams
Osams are still struggling in the bottom and this game didnt make it better. SD2 took a comfortavle 2-0 win on dm2/dm3 and never seem to be shaken in this one. I think SD2 have a good chance at the higher positions this season.

SD2 vs D2
SD2 also faced off vs D2 the past days and eventhough this game was much closer than their osams game they still won it 2-0 on dm2/dm3 once again. D2 has to start showing some skills if they want to avoid the lower part or even relegation to div3 next season. Plast also said "our secret plan to takeover div3 in eql3 is going well" after the game, so thats maybe what they are aiming for. ;)

Zoo vs Jokkmokk.
Here we have one of the topgames this season as these two teams had to make it into three maps to decide the outcome. Jokkmokk started out good and won dm2 with a very close margin but after this it was time for zoo and their infamous cmt3 wich went in one way traffic all the game. With both Jkova and Tmoe back for this one jmk couldnt put up much of a fight. Last map was e1m2 and the zoo train kept on rolling making the third map quite easy. It seems like jmk missed keyplayer mogge for this one and I think he could have mad an asset on e1m2 atleast to make that one much closer. However zoo are very strong right now and jmk should be glad to have taken one point from this game.

Luis vs Osams
Finally osams won a map eventhough gaz was disappointed afterwards as he thought they could have won this game. However luis are strong on dm2 and started out on that one with a crushing blow to osams and won by a large margin. For second map cmt3 got picked and it seems like osams learnt from the zoo game as they won this one with +100 frags. As third map they played e1m2 and the poles upset osams here by winning with a mere 20 frags margin showing that polish clans can win on e1m2 also. "Polski klan wygral e1m2"
Anyway nice to see both luis and d2 have started playing their games now.

FU vs Osams.
Osams continues to be punchbag of div2 and after this 2-0 result vs FU i have a hard time finding clans that osams could beat this season. FU made a strong performance and won dm3 kinda close vs a trailing osams. After this dm2 was played and osams couldnt put up much of a fight after really pushing it to the limit on dm3. Also FU are a strong team on dm2.

The season is turning out much as expected eventhough i so far have misjudged teams like LS and SSC. Only time and games will tell how this is going to end.
