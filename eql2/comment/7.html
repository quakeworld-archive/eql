Intro
The gates for EQL2 will open 2'nd of Januari 2006 at the start of Qhlan9!
We hope that the divisions are as fair as they can be.

Reflection
The only clan we have given a Wildcard to is Dybbuk.
The rest of the clans from eql1 are in that div that the qualification put them.
The qualifications sucked and We are aware and so were the involved clans. For this season we hope that the playoff/qualification system will satisfy all your needs

Some minor problems

New logo
