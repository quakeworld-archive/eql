Division 3 report - part 1

2006-02-04 15:29 by Racersnigeln

Division 3 - "weekly" report - part 1

Many of you probably wonders why my reports in division 3 are late. Here is the deal, I've been quite busy with duelmania, interviews and irlstuff. And the second week only 2games were played, I think - so I chose to NOT write a report after week 2. But, now clans are on fire, and they play every day, so, I decided to make a report now, and one later tonight, so it won't be a huge deal to read like 5000000lines. I hope you understand, and I'm very sorry for the delay.

This is part 1, part 2 will come tonight, and perhaps a part 3 if I'm not finnished in
part 2. Enjoy :>

pige(pai), the former mny star, joined blt, which is necessary for the survival of blt in div3. with pai in the team, they might
be able to win a couple of games. So, get on with the frags BLT!

CMT lost their former starplayer Joulukuu who joined hippushnik again, in division 4B. Why I don't know, perhaps he didn't feel at home with mipa and his crew. Instead Mega joined(Ron_jeremy) and he will give some strength to cmt. Anyhow, there has been 14 games since my
last report, so this is going to take a while to read through.

After four weeks, Shadow minions are leading the division with 19points,
8games played, followed up by Veterans, 18points 7games played. Third in the division is Apocalypse 2006,
with 15points, 8games played.

in my preview I wrote that I thought apocalypse 2006, Veterans and
shadow minions were going to be top 3, if thats true is yet to see, but right now, my preview is correct. My last report was A2k6 vs. Ax3, so I'm going from the first games played till the latest games played.

The drama queens in freedom have now got a new member. The former hellfire and oblivion-star xorcist. Freedom can actually win a dm2 now when they got the superstar flintheart with his motto "dm2 so ez, we only win there"
with the podsmoker xorcist high @ high rl.


First game:
The first gamed played after A2k6 vs. Ax3 is NoName vs. Veterans
The first map was E1M2, it started kind of slow, both teams csed some. But it turned out to be one hell of a game, it was tight, went back and forth. NoName gave a good fight, but Veterans secured their victory with just a few minutes left, ake screamed in ts2 "ut o harva" and dive got quad and made some kabadaboomfrags, ending up as topfragger. Veterans was kinda chocked that NoName put up such a good fight.

Second map was DM2, which also started kind of slow, was pretty tight. mille showed us he also knows how to play, and he requested the quads from ake. Ake gave mille his permission to go crazy and madhax. He got some rls, killed some boomsticks, took armor, laughed at ake
and made veterans win dm2 aswell.

Score: Veterans 2 - 0 NoName



Second game:
The next game we are looking at is Veterans vs. Bad Luck Troopers.
BLT chose DM3, where it went the ways of the veterans. Jagular teamed with mille in ra, stealing quads, making some frags and locked the map. Veterans are on fire now, and the only team that might stop them is sm. Veterans chose DM2, since they practiced against BLT one week before this game, and kicked BLT with 250-25. Veterans thought that it was a ez win before the game even had started. Veterans took control early in the map but were unable to secure low, with bad luck troopers in ra-mega. When the countdown started, there was a 30frag lead for Veterans, and they were able to win DM2 with slowspawning.

Score: Veterans 2 - 0 Bad Luck Troopers.


Third game:
Apocalypse vs. Compot is the next game you are going to read about. A tight game, it all begun with DM3, where spzman ran around like a aids kurwa and collecting frags everwhere.
The russians sure proved that even they can when they want to. The swedes with the minping couldn't stop spzman and lost with two frags, 135-137

Next map was dm2, where A2k, with tGM in the top held tele and collecting quads won. Some russian rat chewed on spzmans connection-line so he timed out. Instead some lamer named des joined, and compot got a warning. A2k won with 191-119

Decider @ e1m2 - spzman was disconnected, with full of hate to that chewing rat, so compot played with uzhas on e1m2 instead of spzman. It was tight on this map aswell, but after wenty full minutes, the russians came out as winners. With their 188-160 score, they took two important points against A2k6. Well played!

Score: Apocalypse 2006 1 - 2 Compot


Forth game:
Team Freedom (drama queens) vs. Veterans
I must say that I'm amazed by team freedom. They are a good team, still they manage to loose. They are a nice gang, with cool and funny players, if only c - the player - could admit he's the dr?ng of the team they could win a couple of games. Anyway, Veterans are perhaps the strongest team in div3, and Ake Vader said on irc, before week 1 started, "I will be very disappointed even if we loose one map against a team in div3". Perhaps they are better off in div2?
The first map - Dm3 was pretty even and the clans score was even aswell. It wasn't before the last penta when Ake snapped it that Veterans could secure the map. Veterans won with 194-174.

E1M2, the second map was pretty ez according to Veterans. They managed to keep gl/ya safe and Freedom couldn't do that much about it. Freedom needs more practice - so keep playing my friends! Veterans won with 278-166

Score: Veterans 2 - 0 Team Freedom (drama queens)


Fifth game:
The finnish players vs the russian players in NoName vs. Clan Metally Troopers
DM3 - hafog is more than a nice guy - he's also a nice player. He ruled Dm3 where it was pretty even. NoName through their super-star cheater ilf managed to keep up good. But, hafog(hfg) lead his comrades to victory - 156 - 115.

DM2 was a rape. Without cheater-ilf in the team, NoName couldn't do anything, and it was a easy victory for CMT. 234 - 86 for cmt.

Score: Clan Metally Troopers 2 - 0 NoName


Sixth game:
The thriller between Shadow Minions vs. Clan Metally Troopers
is the next game. mm is a teamplayer, trying to give his teammates credit, but I must say that he's the one who leads them to victory. I can say that sm is like d2 - sm is mm and tree players, d2 is billy and three players. Shadow Minions play great though, they get mm rl, gets mm quad, let mm frag out rls, lets mm secure the key on the map, take some rls, keeps the key seceure and lets mm frag for them. I might be harsh here, but thats almost the way it is. I really would like to see sm and vets in div2 next EQL. The first map was E1M2, where sm took alot of frags in the first 10-12 minutes, then played easy till the end and won with 208 - 142.

The next map was DM3 where cmt locked the map in the beginning. They had control over quad, pent and ra. But, sm played well and even with the map kinda locked by cmt, they managed to get some frags also. When there was a few minutes left, mm became angry, took rl
and quad, and fragged his way to a secure ra. A nice comeback and with mm's frags, they managed to win by four frags on dm3, 140 - 136. I would wanted cmt to win this game, since I like them, but the better team wins, and thats the way it is.

Score: Shadow Minions 2 - 0 Clan Metally Troopers.

The last game for this part is coming up now.

Seventh game:
the last game in this part is Compot Vs. Ax3.
My polish friend spokz with his 70~ ms played very well. I thought Compot would be a hard opponent to Ax3, especially
since they won vs. A2k, but I was wrong. Ax3 played splendid and won the first map - DM3 - with 175 - 88.

Even the second map - Dm2 - They played great. It was no problem for spokz and his axe team to axe murder some enemies and bring alot of frags home for their team. 283 - 92 to Ax3

Score: Ax3 2 - 0 Compot
