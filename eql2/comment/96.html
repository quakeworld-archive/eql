More inactive teams

2006-02-28 23:40 by term

Division 2 team Luis clan have been removed from eql2 due to inactivity. All theire matches are removed without changeing the div2 standings at all.

Division 1 team Antiquad have asked to be removed as well, they have active members in the team but unfortunately some of them are listed for other teams in eql2, they'll be back in eql3 tho. This means that tVS and Leftovers changes positions in the div1 standings.

Division 3 team Bad Luck Troopers have been removed too. This changes the standings in div3 a bit :(. Veterans and Apocalypse 2006 changes positions as well as Brutality and clan metally troopers.

Division 3 team NoNaMe have been disqualified after theire second WO. This changes the standings in div3 again which is a bit silly. If anyone have suggestions how to do handle this situations better in eql3 please let us know. Anyway the top4 teams are still the same even tho the order amongst them have changed a bit.
