Division 5 is over

2006-03-08 15:29 by peppe

So, I guess that's it. Division five group games are finished...
We have all played and watched exciting games that made som of us scream out loud.
The battle between Clan Cube Squad and Oldones on winning division 5 all came down to the game Suddendeath.Project vs Oldones, where Clan Cube Squad was 2 points ahead of Oldones.

Suddendeath.Project chose dm3 and got an arse-kicking loss there(should have seen Oldones secret tactics coming there).
As the time came for the second map, more specs joined and changed their nicks(funny enough) to "go go sd.p". I suspect nimfo and engineer from CCS.
Thanks to their support we won E1M2 which was Oldones map.
Now, the last map was cmt4(where more funny specs joined) which I think both teams preferred since we have played equal on that map several times(or atleast 2). Suddendeath.Project controlled the map for the bigger half of the game but somewhere around 6:00 Hoder flyes over my shoulders and steals the pent, this is so awesome I will have to make an .avi of it :)
Despite the pent-steal we managed to keep the map somewhat safe, until Oldones started stealing quads and shafting our rls to death. The frags just kept on coming for Oldones and before we knew it, they were in a lead of 15 frags or so. I got the last quad and had 200/200 rl
but neither mli, knast, hoder or Jambba was to be found. All we got was a few frags but slowspawning helped Oldones so they won by 10 frags! Short to say, it was ggs.
And by this, we congratulate Clan Cube Squad on winning division 5!

In the bottom of division 5 we have been waiting for Looser Squadrun2 to win a game, or even a map. Sadly enough this did not happen, ls2 ends up on the 9th place.
Just above ls2 is SamoJedit, what caused them to end up this low is probably inactivity.

Shadow death, this team surprised us and stole a map thanks to sneaky moves by M4r1n3.
They even won against The Gibbers, I hope to see shadow death fighting for it in eql3 because I know they can if they want to.

The Gibbers hasn't been too active either, but I dare to say that they will be top4 next season.
More pracs and they will go further than 6th place.

Vitun Kova Bosse, what happened? haven't seen them play for ages, and this makes me sad because playing vs VKB is always fun. Well, I hope VKB will be aiming for top4 next season. 5h place for Vitun Kova Bosse :F

Suddendeath.Project has played alot this season and finished quite well even though we've only played one season together, I couldn't be more satisfied by the 4th place we got.
The competition was good and better than us.

J?garna has dropped their keyplayers xero, eggarf and und to wunderbar, this is not good for next season but they will probably fight as long as Eipert, Frasse and Edvin is around.
3rd place for a dangerous J?garna that surprised everyone by winning against CCS in the middle of the season.

Oldones, praccing all day long and trying to learn this mm3-stuff. they finished 2nd by bout 500 frags... This was a tough season but Oldones has been playing well all matches and I know they might do the same in div4, if they get there :O

Clan Cube Squad, CONGRATULATIONS! they won division 5 and they sure deserved it, I know these guys are good after seeing them rape div4-clans(not gonna mention what clans).
I expect these guys in div4 and hopefully win there too.
