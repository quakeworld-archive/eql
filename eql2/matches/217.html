#2 2006-01-20 13:16 by gaz

DM3 - steppa got RL, tyrone got quad and an SSC guy got penta. Steppa CS'd like dag himself and after creeping his way around the map was able to take out the SSC RL. Some Osams control followed, but we weren't able to break out of RA side to get a grip on YA. We had a lead, but it was very slim.

Just before 2nd pent, SSC made a takeover and all of our weapons died either going for pent or during the pentrun. SSC took their chance by putting us in the rapecloset and locking the door. They held good control of RA and ran the quads well for the next 12 minutes or so, racking up a lead of over 100. Our boys managed to regain control for the last few minutes and make the score look nicer, but it was way too late.

After having mad ping spikes in prac vs Freedom before this match, my ping had settled down during DM3 so I joined for our map, DM2. Sadly, the stability which reigned while speccing for 20 minutes decided to give up on me about 1 minute into DM2, and tyrone had to replace me. Needless to say, my ping was fine as soon as I was spec again :E

DM2 was fun to watch, with control see-sawing back and forth. SSC had control for the first part of the game and I thought they would take it away. We did a great job of not giving away frags though and restricted their lead. Eventually we took over and held on to a slender lead to make it look quite easy in the end.

We threw CMT4, they threw the QW map and so it was time for a round of Q3 on CMT3. My ping was fine again so I hopped back in and it stayed pretty nice (as nice as 55-65ms can be anyway).
We played pretty well here and had good timing on all of the powerups and the RL, but sadly none of us know the map like we know TB3 so we weren't quite able to put our good teamplay to perfect use. SSC did enough with quads and were sneaky enough around ring/RA to stay in control. It was much closer than the final scores for most of the game and I think it was only 30 frags with 2 minutes to go, before they decided they had had enough of our impertinence and threw us back into the rapecloset. This time they threw away the key.
