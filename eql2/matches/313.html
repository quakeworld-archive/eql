Reports reports reports:

DM3: They got RL, we got pent, I think they got quad but I don't recall exactly. Anyway we did a great job of taking RLs for the first 5 minutes or so and despite not getting a firm lock on the map we had enough control of RA to take quads and do some damage. HHHV used their high div experience to limit the frags we racked up and the game stayed pretty close most of the way through, until we got the last pent and were able to frag a little. Despite how close it was, it never felt like we were going to lose, and we always seemed to have at least 1 RL guy doing a nice job at RA/ring. It was probably our best team performance of the season.

DM2: It was 1 RL each at the start, and buggy got quad, but was killed quickly by gorbatjev with GL. I think it was 2 RLs each quite soon after, but Goljat got the next quad and also quite a lot of quads after that :E He racked up frags quickly and picked us off nicely. We had opportunities to come back, in fact I don't think I've seen a team bore quite so many quads as HHHV did in this game :E Still, we couldn't manage it until 3 minutes were left when Gorbatjev did a really nice defensive move to kill their quad, and aided by lunna and steppa took over the map. We had lock for the last 3 minutes which is why the score finished 205-127 rather than 240-70 :-) Had we been able to take over sooner, or had a better start, we could have been looking at our first win of the season. C'est la vie!

E1M2 felt like FFA. Neither side did a lot of damage with quad and we shared YA control a fair bit. HHHV's individual skills came to the fore and they were able to frag their way ahead quite comfortably. As you can see from the screenshot, I have lost my E1M2-mojo somewhat recently :F
