Big Game, that's the topic for tonight.

Dm3 was on, our warriors getting rl quad ring and ng, just leaving pent 4 the enemies. I myself decided to go dare-mode, and managed to do that pretty well while my teammates ran around getting frags all over the place. This round felt won from the first minute, and obviously, we won.

Dm2...

Fix's extraordinarily poor performance which landed him on -15@dm3 got himself on the bench. Zhinner got in and won the prewargame against fs. If dm3 felt won from the first minute, this one felt lost. They kinda got all the weapons, kotilator got quad and gave them hell, but it sadly wasn't enough. Matlock was due and we did not have any counter for it.

Decider, e1m2.

The game felt quite hectic with control switching every now and then, Bostream reduced my adsl to a 14.4k modem at crucial points and the bestest player ends last, cos sometimes, dead is better.
