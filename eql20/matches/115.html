It's really great to still have such dedicated players in a 19-year-old game :). The top4 clans this season were really good, and the teams in the B play offs aren't as far behind as they sometimes might think. In addition it brings a smile to my face when I see teams like Fatal Error and D99 really trying to improve: practicing, going through tactics, creating better mm2 messages etc.

This final wasn't quite as tight as I anticipated, but it was great fun anyway. Dm3 was pretty solid play from us as a team. Not many unnecessary packs left and communication was pretty solid. Dm2 was way more chaotic, and personally I played pretty bad. Luckily the team was there to save the day! E1M2 felt really good right from the start and we kept things under control a lot better than in our group stage game. Razor seemed to play a good e1m2 and it never seizes to amaze me how he can always be in such a good shape even though he almost never seems to play :).

Good games and see you next season!
