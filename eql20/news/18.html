Final week of playoffs!

2015-12-14 08:37 by Hooraytio

The time is up for the semifinals and we move on to the final week of EQL 20!

Unfortunately 3B couldnt field 4 players and thus SD moves on to meet FOM in the final of the A playoffs. FOM have the chance to win EQL for the 4th time while SD have two previous wins in the books. I think SD can take this home 3-2 but FOM always have Milton who can make almost anything happen.

Should 3B get their team back on track they have the possibility to redeem themselves a little bit in the bronze game vs C88. Perhaps C88 can take revenge here from the group stage where 3B managed a 2-1 win.

In the B playoffs 020 will take on SD2 in the final and CSN will face off against FU in the bronze game. SD2 has been looking really strong and if they have their best lineup I think they can prove to be too much for 020. Anyway, i think 5 maps will be needed as Im going with 3-2 for SD2.

CSN has been a stable contender for the div2/b-final title during the last few seasons so missing the B final was a setback for them. They have a good playoff record against FU but in the group game FU managed to take them down 2-0. Time for revenge for CSN? Im inclined to guess 3-2 for CSN but FU could just as well win this.

LB managed to squeeze past FE in the semifinal of the C playoffs and 3-0 for LB doesnt tell the whole story. FE had plenty of chances on all 3 maps but crazymac turned out to be the deciding factor for LB.

In the other semifinal Paras won 3-0 after a really close E1M2 against D99R. Looks like D99R really improved from the start of the season.

The final will be very exciting as Paras will meet LB, a matchup we missed during the regular season. I think this game will go the distance as both teams looked good in their respective semifinals. Paras are looking good for a 3-2 win here.

In the bronze game I see FE winning 3-0 as they have more routine and better players than D99R. E1M2 might be the map where D99R can do some damage.


All games are BO5 and the deadline is Dec 20th.

Good luck!

/Hooraytio
