Match and map rules

2016-02-09 08:47 by Hooraytio

I'll put the map and match rules here so they are a bit easier to find.

Regular season

The regular season will start today and end on April 10th.

All teams in the respective divisions meet once in a best of three game. In a game each team pick their own map and if a decider is needed there will be either a TB3, a tossing procedure or an agreement between the clans. See picking procedure below.

The map pool will be DM2, DM3, E1M2, CMT1b, CMT3, CMT4 as decied by votes from the clans. The D99 special team did not get a vote as they already got votes in their regular teams. SD2 and FOM also missed the vote because they joined too late.


Picking maps in regular season games


TB3 case

If both teams played a TB3 map and the game is tied 1-1 you have two options. Either you play the last TB3 as decider or you play a map both clans can agree on.


Mixed case

If one team played a TB3 map and the other played a CMT and the score is 1-1 you have two options. Either you toss one map each from the TB3 maps and play the remaining one as decider or you play a map both clans can agree on.


CMT case

If both teams played a CMT map and the score is 1-1 you have three options.
1. You toss one map each from the TB3 pool and play the remaining one as decider.
2. You toss one map each from the CMT pool and play the remaining one as decider.
3. You play a map both clans can agree on as decider.

If you cant agree wich pool to toss from then the toss will be from the TB3 pool.

Both picking and tossing order can be decided with the cnd rnd command on the server if you cant agree who shoud pick or toss first. Both teams should have picked a map Before the first map is started.


Map picks in the playoffs

All games are best of 5. The first 3 maps will be TB3 and if more maps are needed the team trailing 1-2 will pick a map from the whole pool. If a 5th map is needed the team who had a 2-1 lead will pick the last map from the whole pool.
