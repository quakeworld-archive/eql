Predictions Div 5

2006-03-24 00:29 by fer

Hello fellow quakers, I am going to attempt to write a column on division 5 this season, however im in the same boat as murdoc and have not written anything for the quake scene before. Please be gentle with your comments!
Anyway lets kick things off with some hazy predictions for the upcoming season in division 5. Unfortunatly there are a few clans here with unknown players to me so please dont slam me for my mistakes. I see there are few clans in this division with a wide range of skills levels. This will make for some "interesting" games!

Clans:

Zundbyberg aZZ KickerZ
The Outcasts Clan
Clan Cube Squad

J?garna


Zundbyberg aZZ KickerZ
------------------------
There is quite a lot of talk about this clan being in the wrong division. I think with their strongest team they will win this division quite comfortably.
Key Players: znappe, almeida
Prediction: Winners

The Outcasts Clan
------------------------
Having seen this clan play once I was very impressed with one player, azz1d. I hear that he was a top q3ctf player and it really shows in his aim. I issue a warning to other clans in this division. Keep him away from quad!
Key Players: azz1d
Prediction: Near the top

Clan Cube Squad
------------------------
Seemed to play well last season, however just missed a division 4 spot on frag difference. I think these guys are gonna fight really hard to get promoted this season.
Key Players: Tanker
Prediction: Near the top

Drastic Collision
------------------------
I really dont know how well this clan will perform, but from what I remember Iron is pretty good. Well if its the same Iron I have played in the past. :)
Key Players: Iron?
Prediction: Mid Table

Fakin' Idiots
------------------------
Cant say much about this clan as the only player I have heard of is anza. However I will say a mid table finish.
Key Players: ???
Prediction: Mid Table

Loser Squadron 2
------------------------
A poor performance last season but with some new additions to the clan hopefully they can do better this season.
Key Players: ???
Prediction: Mid to Bottom

Shadow Death
------------------------
These guys are great! Nothing seems to get them down. I have seen them get owned by div4 clans but they keep coming back for more. I think they will do better this season and possibly with a late push they will possibly end in the mid table area.
Key Players: Creature
Prediction: Low

The Gibbers
------------------------
Again another clan with more additions and a stronger lineup. However I feel they will struggle since the competition is stronger this season.
Key Players: ???
Prediction: Low

The Pimps
------------------------
I have played a few pracs against these guys and they seem to have some solid teamplay. Not too sure how they will perform but I will say a high finish.
Key Players: Flamer
Prediction: Near the Top

Vitun Kova Bosse
------------------------
No Kip this season and I think they will struggle. Especially since the competition has gone up a notch. Still time for some late additions though. :)
Key Players: Itsinen
Prediction: Low

Mori2ri
------------------------
As an attempt to get mor more :) active we are trialing having mor1 and 2 for eql. There is potential in this clan to do well however there might be problems getting the best lineup for all games.
Key Players: Atrophy, Jacobite
Prediction: Mid Table

J?garna
------------------------
I played these guys a few seasons ago and pleased to see a lot of the same players in the team. Quite a few changes to the team. A mid table finish is in order.
Key Players: Melkor
Prediction: Mid Table
