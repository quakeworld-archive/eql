EQL3 Playoff soon

2006-04-26 01:03 by term

To qualify for a higher divison in EQL4 a team must be top2 in the regular EQL3 season, bottom2 teams will be moved down one division. This will hopefully motivate teams to be more active and it means that we can have a PLAYOFF IN EACH DIVISION!.

Top4 in each division or conference will play this playoff. For Div1, Div3 and Div5 it'll work like the previous Premiership playoff, ie: #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3. Here's a schedule to help you:

Week 7: 2006-05-01 - 2006-05-07
Last week for Div2 and Div4 regular season matches. We have changed the schedule in Div2b a bit, it was wrong from the start and you will have one week less now :(.

Week 8: 2006-05-08 - 2006-05-14
Last week for Div1, Div3 and Div5 regular season matches. Div2 and Div4 Quarterfinals, ie: A#1 vs B#4, A#2 vs B#3, A#3 vs B#2 and A#4 vs B#1.

Week 9: 2006-05-15 - 2006-05-21
Semifinals in all divisions

Week 10: 2006-05-22 - 2006-05-28
Finals in all divisions
