Poll on QW summer break

2006-04-28 17:46 by ?ke Vader

After realizing that the old poll had been up for like seven years i decided to come up with a new survey. This time we want to know how you plan your QW career to come along during the summer. Are y going to relax on the beach, do some job or maybe work up some madskills in time for the next EQL season? Tell us in the comments please!

The last poll was started by Zanne on 2006-03-29 and had the following question and results:

How long should 1 season be in EQL4?
72% - As it is right now (8 weeks)
17% - Doubled, 2 matches vs each clan
11% - (16 weeks) As in prosports (4 months - 2 weeks playoff)

A total of 339 votes was cast.
