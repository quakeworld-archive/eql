Inactive teams

2006-05-02 10:30 by term

Div2B team Loser Squadron, Div4A team Terror Forge, Div4B team smackthatass and Div5 team Drastic Collision have been removed from EQL3 due to inactivity. The standings are intact since none of these teams have played any matches.

Div3 team Wings of Steel have been disqualified after theire second WO. Theire 3 played matches are removed with minor changes in the Div3 standings.

Div2A team Death Dealers have asked to be removed as well, they have only played one match but removing it sent Triada to a relegation position in Div2A.

Please remember that this is the last week for regular season matches in Div2 and Div4, all voided matches will be removed on Sunday 2006-05-07 at 23:59CET.
