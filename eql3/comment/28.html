Newbie Division in EQL4 ?

2006-05-05 11:51 by zanne

I was thinking after reading a few threads about this on qw.nu, perhaps we'll have a Newbie Division in the next season of EQL. There would be some really hard rules though, as follows:

A clan playing in the newbie division may contain of only unknown players that are truley new to QuakeWorld

A clan playing in the newbie division may only do so for one season, if the clan survives and is not dropped dead after the season they will automatlicly advance to Division 5.

If a clan should play with any faker Im sure we could spot this and will ban that clan and the people in it from EQL without any first warnings.

This would give the new people to QuakeWorld a chance to get started without having to know some pro to get through it, ofcourse there will always be a clan winning and a clan losing the division, but if we're lucky, no clan will be a dominating clan.

Please post your comments and thoughts about this idea.
