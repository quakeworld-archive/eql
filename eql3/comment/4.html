Predictions Div 1

2006-03-22 16:18 by murdoc

Hello boys and girls, welcome to a new eql season. I am gonna write my predictions about div1 this season. This is my first time writing anything about the qw scene so don't attack me on that. Divison 1 sure looks hot this season, alot of hot fights coming up. Hope you'r gonna like it and have fun reading :)


Clans:

1 Antiquad
2 Clan MalFunction
3 Firing Squad
4 Fragomatic
5 Koff
6 LegeArtis
7 Slackers
8 Suddendeath
9 the Viper Squad


1 AntiQuad
------------------------
The "new" Antiquad doesn't look so strong as they were before. With there 2 new players Darkki and Bamsen from div2. They needed these new players because of the inactivity of pinevester this season. Harvester was one of there key players. There is alot of prac needed to even win a map from the others.

My prediction: 8th place.


2 Clan MalFunction
------------------------
I heard fifi is back and angua is done with army in just a couple of weeks (4/5?) so with the old fifi/angua/fix lineup there shouldn't be a problem to win some maps/games.

My prediction: 5th place.


3 Firing Squad
------------------------
Still one off the strongest teams, as they showed in EQL2 by losing the finals with close games 3-1 vs LA. With there super combo reppie/razor they are hard to take out. Now when spoinka is back they need some more prac.

My prediction: 4th place.


4 Fragomatic
------------------------
They where very inactive as a team, have seen them play some mix games, and some clan games. But they still look as one of the favourites to win eql. Very strong linup.

My prediction: 1st place


5 Koff
------------------------
Koff :) they impressed me on how good they were. Solid teamplay nice guys fun to play with. Very strong dm3 but they need more prac on the other maps to make a chance.

My prediction: 6th place


6 LegeArtis
------------------------
Now when mutilater joined LA, they are gonna even be stronger then the season before. Very hard to beat on e1m2. They are aiming for the finals for sure.

My prediction 2nd place.


7 Slackers
------------------------
Former Leftovers, with paradoks added. The most active div1 clan atm. Should be able to win most of there games.

My prediction: 3rd place.


8 Suddendeath
------------------------
I have seen less activity of this clan wich they really need to even win a map. Maybe they should take phrenic back in?

My prediction: 9th place.


9 the Viper Squad
------------------------
Strong team good teamplay, but are the strong enough without milton or will the be even stronger now when goljat has joined their forces?

My prediction: 7th place.
