Hi, long time no see ;)

Have been a while now since we played 4on4 in a tourney.
With all of you guys back we hope that we can have as active tourney as we had back in eql3!

The registration starts 4 of September (Monday) and EQL4 starts 18 of September (Monday).

Why we start this late? Lots of the players are students and they begin their studies in the start of september and will hopefully find a clan before that!

We hope that you all can spread this news to the major sites so we can spread it around that we have started up again.

Much love /EQL-crew
