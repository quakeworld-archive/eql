EQL Season 3 registration open

2006-03-13 22:57 by ?ke Vader

With a slight delay the registration for the third season of this lovely league has now opened. The procedure is just like previous seasons, just go to the sign up page and fill in the form. After that you're supposed to login and add the players that forms your team. When you've done that the next step is to discuss what maps your clan would like to vote for this season. This time we only got two options to vote for - it's either TB3 only or TB3 + CMT3 & CMT4.

As you may know the remaining games in EQL2 are to be played this week. All games that haven't been played when this week is over will be removed. This is because the registration for EQL3 will be open one week and closes this upcoming sunday (March 19). After that (on Monday) the new season will kick off immediately and it's all rock'n'roll from there on!
