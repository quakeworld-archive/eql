Ideas for EQL4

2006-06-05 21:31 by term

I like Ake's idea regarding a fixed schedule and there are probably several different solutions, what about this one?

First off we do not play on Fridays & Saturdays. Of the remaining five weekdays each team picks a primary, secondary and third day that suits them. Each team also picks a time that suits them best, say either 19:00, 20:00 or 21:00CET. This is all done when you sign up (where u vote maps) and once the schedule is made you may not change this.

Then we (or rather some script) make the schedule. There will always be a weekday that suits both teams, and if too many teams chose Sunday as theire primary day we could use some modifications to spread the days. Regarding the time a mean value must be most fair.

An example:
csn picks Sun > Thu > Tue @ 20:00CET
sdp picks Mon > Wed > Tue @ 21:00CET
IE theire match will be scheduled Tue @ 20:30CET


Then I have an idea regarding WO's too:

If none of the teams have players at the scheduled time then the result will be 0-0, maybe we could have one backup day scheduled as well but I dunno if that is such a good idea.

If only one team have four players they need to prove that in order to get WO by joining a server and take a screenshot with theire four players and a spectator (prefereable an EQL-admin) present to confirm it.

Just an idea.. let us know what you think please.

Div5 final over

2006-06-01 12:34 by sassa

=tG= vs ZaK

Yesterday we saw the final in division 5, both teams are allready through to division 4 next season but a final is never wrong is it? :-).

First map was The Gibbers, dm2, they got a pretty nice start and held the map throughout the game, we saw a few exceptions when Znappe managed to sneak in and kill a some RL's but it was never a close.

Second map was ZaK's arena, dm3, =tG= got the first pent and the second RL, the game was never really a close but the scores indicates other, we saw wonderful shafting by ZaK and a great TP from The Gibbers.

Third map was ZaK's choice, cmt4, they had to win the map but it looked like The Gibbers handled the situation well, even though we saw an amazing quad steal and retake of YA by, again, Znappe in the middle of the match, noone doubted the fact that this was going to be the final map.

All in all, Great game, and congratulations The Gibbers!

PS. dont forgett Sundays div1 final between LegeArtis and Slackers @ 19:00 CET
