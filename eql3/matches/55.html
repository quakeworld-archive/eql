Match report:

After some pathetic trying to get EQL match tonight we finally succeeded when 3tk came for asking a match, so there we went.

I have no idea whose choise was DM3 or DM2 since we couldn't play the same map twice, we agreed on those.

So the first map was DM3. We got a real good start and we took the map under our control (somewhat) and got hefty lead. Then harsi timed out and vert had to join the game since harsi didn't show up. 3TK started playing that time and we just tried to hang on there and won by a few frags in the end.

The second map was DM2 and harsi showed up again. We got pretty good start again and we kinda kept the water under our control the whole round. There were few times when we lost it but somehow they couldn't keep it either and we managed to get most of the quads and kept the water/tele pretty safe and won by a hefty margin.

I have to say 3TK was the nicest clan we played against so far. They actually broke the game when harsi timed in the beginning of first DM3 (so our DM3 score was from the latter round). If they didn't, we might have lost that round since they got pretty good start.

GGs 3tk, your sportsmanship is world class. GL in the future, too.
