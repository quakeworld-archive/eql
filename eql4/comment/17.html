Div1 preview by razor and reppie

2006-09-28 20:07 by razor

Prediction (razor):
1st: LA
2nd: SR
3rd: KOFF
4th: FS
5th: CMF
6th: SD
7th: AQ
8th: SSC
9th: VETS
10th: DR

Prediction (reppie):
1st: KOFF
2nd: LA
3rd: SR
4th: FS
5th: CMF
6th: SD
7th: AQ
8th: VETS
9th: DR
10th: SSC

Legeartis (razor):
Yes I think they will take the first spot this time but with SR and Koff being very short behind.
IF they can get their best players playing and get some good teamplay going with their new and cool lineup
with xantom. Nabbe will probably be saving the day for them in many situations, and riker will make some
frenzy kamikaze attacks that will put down the forces of SR. With also some luck included.
If dag plays in playoff again and he gets in shape they have a good chance of winning the whole thing as well.
But the opposition in playoff will be tougher!
La always had a very wide player base and with alot of skill, and no change this time with also shiva playing
regularly again. They have the winner attitude when it matters and that is what makes them win this time!


Koff (reppie):
Gamer, Jukka, Eh and Scenic. Together with SR probably the most active clan in div1 at the moment. They play
like a team perfectly, because of their good use of mm3 and probably simply because they play together so much.
This teamplay combined with the individual skill of especially Gamer makes Koff really hard to beat.
Last season they lost quite some maps with just a few frags, and just seemed a bit unlucky at times :) So with a
little less bad luck i think Koff will get very far this season.


Slackers (razor):
One of the best teams ofcourse, who I think will grab the 2nd spot but with much potential to take 1st spot
as well. I think they will win vs Koff this time even though KOFF has been looking very strong and probably
the strongest during the pre-prac season. But Koff will lack the skill of luck vs SR I am afraid.
They have a very strong and stabile lineup with paradoks,murdoc,insane,dragon and also krab who is getting
alot better. They have the sneaky lg-tactic to RA and it seems to fit very good for them cause they all have
awesome LG's. Even insa on 50 ms :) So everyone should fear these bastards.


Firing Squad (reppie):
After a really crappy, inactive last season, we're hoping to play a good season this time :) Gem/1-2-3 have
been replaced with Keyser and hopefully Blixem, and with some prac i think/hope we could make it to the semi-finals.


Clan MalFunction (reppie):
Winners of last NQR season. CMF always seemed to end up high in tournaments, regardless of their lineup and activity.
This season however, they might just need to much time to get in shape again and we think they will lose too many points
in the first games to make it to the semi-finals. Fix, Fifi, Jus and Angua are deffinetly not to be underestimated though
and i wouldn't be suprised if they end up winning the whole thing anyway :)


Veterans (razor):
One of the newcomers in DIV1 this season and I can?t say I know much about them yet, but I watched some pracs
and they can probably make an upset scorewise vs the best clans but will have very tough time to win.
Vs the bottom clans they can probably get a win. I am not sure what their top line up is,
Ake vader, Its4real, daan and mogge perhaps. Daan had the scariest aimbot LG I have seen in a while so look
out SR and all the other ones in the povdmm4 club. Will be very interesting to see what the new clans can do.


Antiquad (reppie):
Haven't seen them play much so far. Last season didn't go very well for them, maybe because of the loss of Diki,
who mostly played a pretty vital role in their games. I think AQ often causes suprises though, so watch them
carefully :) And if you play them don't forget to take all the grenade launchers, cause you should know you're
screwed if you don't.


Satanic Slaughter Clan (reppie):
Don't know much about them, just that they were in Div2, then Div1, then Div2 and now back to Div1. Perhaps they are
too good for div2, but just not good enough for div1 .. but tbh i don't know at all :)
More SSC (razor):
I dont know much about SSC either, except that milton is really good and can probably put any clan out of balance,
does he have the right teammates to back him up? I dont have a clue, but perhaps, they have at least played very long
together i think? SSC is to look out for.


Suddendeath (razor):
SD seem to have a new lineup this season with the famous sassa-cam taking a break? Mille, Molle, Mulle and co:
will probably make a good job as well though. And SD which still is using MM3 I assume have a chance of making
an upset here and there. But I think they have a bit of too new lineup with new div1 players that will probably
get a tough time, but you never know. And also they won a stabile game vs CMF already which proves they are to
be reckoned with, even though CMF probably is heavily out of shape right now.
But blaps and his boys will do good I think but not enough to get to one of the 4 playoff spots.


Dead Rabbits (razor):
Another newcomer in Div1 who has some experienced players like Darklord, Interceptor etc.
I haven?t seen inter played so much lately though, not at all actually. But they have an interesting lineup with
lots of decent players. But that?s just it, they will only be decent this season and it won?t be enough for div1
this time. But this could very well be a darkhorse considering they are a bit of a sleeping lion which COULD
do good and surprise. They for sure are going to be able to compete with many clans in div1, but I think they will
end up taking the last spot afterall, bad luck or whatever may cause it, but thats how it will be! ;)
Perhaps phrenic and co: proves me wrong.
