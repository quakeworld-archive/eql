div1 preview by Insane

2006-09-30 15:54 by Goqsane

Finally, a great Division 1 preview has arrived, written by the great man himself - Insane.

As it`s a very lazy sunday i thought "huh do something for the community maybe?". So here it is my own div1 predictions!!
Looking at clan list we can see some new clans in division 1 (Vets, Rabbits and SSC) also there are few clans missing FoM
(what`s with manu these days?), tVS and GTG.
So we have 10 clans lets take a closer look on them shall we ?:d

Koff:
One of the title contenders imo. Koff have a very strong and stable lineup combined that with great mm3 usage and a lot of
practise they will definetly go far. The question is if they can play as good in official games as in pracs? Scenic and Gamer
are names everyone know but they also have Eh who`s like the most underrated player ever.
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 1-3

Suddendeath:
This is one of the clans that are based more on good teamplay than on individual talents. They have a bit new lineup this
season since my best all time friend Sassa (:e) is inactive they tend to play with bps, ok98, mille and molle. There`s not
much i can say about this team apart of they are using mm3 and they are doing it well so with a bit of luck and good start
they might take a map here and there.
Best map: dm2
Final spot: 5-6

Slackers:
What can i say. We`re aiming for the top spot like every season but can we make it? I think one of our strongest sides are
our stable team (we play with para murdoc dragon and me like 80% of the games) + mm3 and of course the sr luck factor :ee
I think we have a good balance between defensive (me, drag. krabby) + offensive (murdoc + para) players and we are active
plus we have a secret weapon teh upcoming qw star Krabby! Ph33r!
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 1-3

Veterans:
A newcomer to division 1 and frankly they will get a hard life here. Looking at their lineup you might think they will be
easy meat but no! I think they`re capable of taking some maps from weaker clans. Daan and Ake are good players and they
should not be underestimated.
Best map: e1m2
Final spot: 8-10

Dead Rabbits:
Another new clan in division 1. They dont have any league history so far since they`ve been formed few months ago. Looking
at the lineup you can see some well known names in there ( Darklord, Grisling, jOn, Purity ) but the question is if they
can turn that into real teamplay because you need that to do something in div 1. I dont think they will be a match for top
teams this season but maybe next ? who knows :) I wish them good luck
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 8-10

Antiquad:
Here`s a finnish clan that have been in division 1 for ages now. Can they make it far this season? Dont think so. They will
miss teh starplayer Diki for sure! They havent been praccing at all during the last weeks what makes them one of they weakest
clans in division 1 atm.
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 6-9

Lege Artis:
Ahh the famous LA. As every other season they have to be threated like the title contenders this season aswell. With names well
known among the community they can go very far. They have a strong lineup consisting 11 players altho i think the Nabbe, Riker,
Xantom and Memil is the best combination atm what they need is more practise and mm3 imo. They can be deadly on every map
but e1m2 is by far their best.
Best map: e1m2
Final spot: 1-3

Firing Squad:
Firing Squad the team with an unstoppable duo of Rep and Raz. They have the potential thats sure. Reppie, Razor Spoink and Legser
are a good balanced lineup. There`s only one problem. They don`t prac at all! Come on guys put a little more effort :d
These guys have been playing together for a very long time and thats very important for the teamplay i think that with some
practise they can get to playoffs.
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 3-5

Clan Malfunction:
The alltime top finnish clan. I always liked Cmf. I like their style, their attitude and i like the players aswell. Unfortunately
they`ve been very inactive during last few months and that puts them in a bad place for reaching the playoffs, surely they have
the quality but even their first game vs suddendeath (which they`ve lost) proved they wont go far without any practise.
Fix, Fifi , Angua and Hlt is a great lineup capable of winning the whole thing theres only this small question do they want to?
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 3-5

Satanic Slaughter Clan:
Milton`s boys are back again in Division 1. They are that kind of a clan that can make an upset vs everyone. If it`s Milton`s
day everyone should be carefull :D Milton and Skisso are their top players and with a good support from others + mm3 they shouldnt
have problems with winning few maps here and there.
Best map: dm3
Final spot: 6-9

Table after regular season:
1. Koff
2. Sr
3. La
4. Cmf
5. Fs
6. Sd
7. SSc
8. Aq
9. Vets
10. Rabbits
