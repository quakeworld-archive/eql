The moment has finally come!

2006-09-04 22:09 by ?ke Vader

The summer is over and it?s finally time to get some gibbing going. The EQL crew is back in business and we have a new season ahead of us to look forward to. In this first article i?m going to bring up what changes you?re up for and what you can expect from EQL this season.

First of all i must say that it's nice to see that quite a few clans have raised their activity level a bit, among both elite clans and the lesser skilled clans. Some people have said that this is the first summer that there have been significantly less clans active in the scene. I really hope most of the clans (and why not a few new ones?) will show up and play in EQL this season, and if not this season then they?re very welcome the next one. Last season ended with two very heavy W.O. victories in the final rounds of the division 1 playoff and that's something we all don't want to see happen again as it is very sad to not get the action you would expect from such great clans.

In time for this season i've tried to improve the site in a few areas. I made some work in the beginning of the summer so i might have forgotten about some of the differences you'll see. Most of the optimization has been on the admin side of things so i don't think there will be any big change for you. Anyway, there have been changes on the ruleset side of things too and the major thing being the decision to implement a bit of a more fixed schedule this season. We made a survey on the site as you may know that was along the lines of In EQL4 I would like to play matches on... (more info in Ideas for EQL4) and the result was:
47% - Any day in any week that it happend to suit 7 other guys

38% - One of 3 fixed days every week (not Fri or Sat unless both teams agree)

16% - On 1 fixed day every week (not Fri or Sat unless both teams agree)

Total votes: 219

This wasn?t any final vote however, it was just to see if a change would get accepted among the players. While the old system got the most votes, we believe that it would be interesting with a change to see if it (hopefully) has a nice effect when it comes to the amount of played games in time. Our goal with this change is that we don't want to see clans play all of their 7 games or so the last week of the season. We want the standings to be accurate to how the clans have played so far, just like in any other competitive acitivity.

With this we could also have a page on the site that shows upcoming games with dates and even times (even if some games will be delayed due to various pingwhine of course, but you'll still know that they will play) when they?re going to battle it out. It will be easier for the spectating player to know when he's going to buy his julmust and ostb?gar and tune in Sassa on teamspeak. The only downside to this being that clans might have to make QW a bit more of a priority of course. I don?t think it will be that great of a change for the clans though ? just make sure you have enough free players!

To pull this off we have made the decision to let clans supply us with at least three occasions in the week that are the most suitable for them. You do that when you log in with your team account and add your players e.t.c. In that menu it says "add weekdays and times", just add the ones most suitable to you. When we later on generate the schedule we will take these days and times into concern to make the schedule as good as possible. If things still doesn?t fit between two clans then it will be possible to reschedule ? but only one week forward and you have to do it one week before the game is on!

Enough rambling about, now it's time for you to sign up for this league and get some practice games going so you can shape up in time for the start at the 18th of september. If you notice any weird errors on the site then please comment on it in this news item. If you don't find any errors, then comment anyway!

Let's rock'n'roll!
