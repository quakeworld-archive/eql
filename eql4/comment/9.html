T-minus two days until launch

2006-09-16 14:37 by ?ke Vader

It's soon time for kick off in the European Quake League and i'm afraid i have some sad news about some things we won't see in EQL this season.

If you have read my first news post then you know that we promised a fixed schedule this season of EQL. I can now tell you that this won't happen due to real life business taking too much time from me. It's a combination of school, bad planning and lazyness from my side that caused this. This also means that we won't have any kind of fragstats this season, which would've been fun since that's a feature we are really lacking. I apologize for all this and hope that you will enjoy the league anyway.

Despite all this we can hopefully find some comfort in the good news this season - the fact that we have no less than 62 clans who will battle it out in EQL4. That's about five less than last season but even if the amount of signed teams is lower i hope that the amount of teams who played all their games and didn't drop/wasn't kicked is higher when this season is over. It doesn't matter if your team is signed on a list if you won't play your games, right?

I now hope that you're all practicing like mad to become the next EQL champion and i wish you the best of luck and that many fullstarts shall help you on the road to victory!

Update
Don't forget to make your voice heard in the map vote! When you login with your team then it says "votes" in the team menu. Select the map vote poll and choose your desired option!
