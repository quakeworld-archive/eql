Divisions

2006-09-10 16:06 by term

We have started to make the divisions and could use feedback on some teams. At the moment we have too few newbie teams to make a 6th division. All divisions are a bit preliminary since teams still can add players to their rosters. If any division gets larger than 16 teams then we will split it.

Spare us the "WTF is team A doing in div X" comments, constructive criticism please. And try to write one post with all your ideas.

We are mostly interested in where to place all the new teams who did not participate in EQL3. The EQL3 teams will be placed according to their results in the EQL3 regular season if they have roughly the same lineup.

Regarding Div1 we have preliminary chosen to put both Vets and SSC there in order to get more than 8 teams. We are a bit afraid of closing Div1 and although neither Vets nor SSC are too eager to play there we feel that if we don't force top Div2 teams to play in Div1 next season then we will end up with a watered-down Div1 quite soon.

Team Freedom have got a wildcard to Div2 due to Valla. dQM will be placed in Div3 (in EQL3 dQM* was Div2 and dQM was Div4, with only two members left from the Div2 team we feel that Div3 is correct). TOC have recruited 3 former TG members and will get a wildcard to Div4 due to this. NO! have added quake (unknown) to their roster and will get a wildcard to Div3. Cmt have lost mega to inactivity so they will get a wilcard back to Div3 (they will not be allowed to add him later).
