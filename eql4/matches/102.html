#3 2006-11-06 10:32 by Mingle (195.148.156.XXX)

Here is the little report of this match as I promised.

dm2: First map. 3tk took dm2, cause they thought that we needed practise in that one :) First 4-5 minutes it was totally FFA I think and the game was tight. Then we locked the map for 7-8 mins and got 45 frags lead. 3tk took the map back and started to control ng-quad-tele area and came down rl with quad. We tried to get killed as less than we could and try to get some control of the map. Quadrunners managed to get most of the down rl's so we didn't get chance to take map back. I got one quad when there was 3 minutes left and we had 15 frags lead, but 3tk managed to kill me at quad as soon as I got it. There wasn't much to do and 40secs before end the game was tight and 3tk fragger those 12 frags in last 40secs. GG

e1m2: Our map. 3tk lost e1m2 vs a2k so we decided to choose this map against 3tk. I think we controlled the map in the begining and lead over 30 frags. 3tk broke our lock and they fragged more than we did in the middle of the map. They got 20 frags lead before 3mins the end. I'm not sure what happened really, but I think joulukuu got few quads and managed to kill most of the rl enemies. When map was left only 40secs joulukuu got last quad and we was 6-7 frags behind. All 3tk members was at RL and bandit got killed at there. He put msg that all enemies are there. Joulukuu attacked there with quad and killed the enemies there (and almost bored his rl also;) ) After the attack the time run out and the game was tight. So 5mins overtime was ahead. Domin said after the game that the last 5mins they played like FFA and they didn't got any control of the map anymore. We didn't much better, but we managed to get more frags than they did. I tried to keep RL safe few minutes so they didn't get many rl's anymore in the last 5 mins. we won the map with 14 frags.

dm3: We got 1st rl but miroslav was so nervous so lg enemy suprised him at ra. he tried to run away but enemy telefragger him :D I managed to get 2nd rl in front of enemy pent but got killed very soon. The game was pretty tight but they got all the time few frags more than we did. There wasn't long map locks from either. And damn i gotta tell Domins lucky telefrag when I got lg-quad and some armors left. As soon as I tried to get RA back he got me with his lucky telefrag. Damn I hate those. But 3tk was this time better in dm3 and won it pretty clearly.

GG's 3tk. We got some taste of those possible semifinal matches to div2 ;P Let's hope we don't meet there then :D
