Sign up for EQL5

2007-02-24 01:09 by term

The registration for European Quake League season 5 is now open. We will close it on Sunday the 11th of March at 23:59CET and start EQL5 on Monday the 12th. Thanks to our new php-coder and admin Premortem, we will have fragstats in EQL5!

We know that many players would like to finish NQR10 before this but there is simply not enough weeks left before the summer to do that. Our goal is 40 teams in 5 divisions and we hope that it is realistic.

New this season is a preliminary schedule based on each team choosing 3 weekdays that suits them. The final goal is a fixed schedule but for now it will be used as a guideline. Teams should however always re-schedule matches using the team-login so that we have an up to date schedule!

Fakeshaft/truelightning will be allowed.
