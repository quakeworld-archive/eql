Semifinal W.O.s

2007-05-27 01:13 by ?ke Vader

As some clans seems to have gone inactive, some teams have made it to the final by default wins. The biggest news is that the division 1 grand final is scheduled for tomorrow (sunday).

The season is coming to an end with summer break knocking at the door. Hopefully there will be some kind of activity during the summer too, i haven't heard anything from the NQR crew nor have we discussed any tournament in the EQL crew. Maybe a third party can step up and arrange some summer kenya tournament for the brave ones that want to play somewhat regularly in the summer?

Anyway, there are still a few matches left to be played and as all the semifinals should have been played until the 20th of May, we are a bit behind schedule. We have therefor decided that Chosen receives a w.o. win against Paranoia as Paranoia seems to have a hard time to play the game and are kind of inactive. Chosen has stepped up and said they will play the final game against Ballistic if they get the final spot.

In division 1 Dota-Allstar-Guys receives a win against Antiquad as the finlanders have had a hard time to fit the game into their schedule. This has been known for some days among the concerned clans and the grand final between Slackers and Dota-Allstar-Guys is scheduled for tomorrow (Sunday) 27th of May at 21.30CEST. There are news to read on both Quakeworld.nu and ESReality about the game. Make sure you are there as this is the last big game for the season!
