#10 2007-05-30 21:30 by plast (83.13.196.XXX)

assassins have lately become my favourite swedish clan - they agree to play late (when we got most bearable pings), they don't whine, they don't delay games, they don't have average 13ms ping, they ain't mean or even rude, they just come, play and say gg <3

we expected this game to be very hard for several reasons, first of all serox the nasty brit was still unavailable, avenger just returned from 9th month army survival several days ago, moltas just bought a new monitor yesterday (switching from crt to lcd is never easy!), keyser turned qw tonight for the first time in more than a month and i haven't played with ping different than 25ms since our last eql game (middle of april), also the overall shape is somewhat always different in the end of the may/summer than during the regular season... so eventually having only one really good player (blasp aka daub) we just hoped that assassins are gonna have similiar dilemmas so it could get even...

but then, suddenly ;), it turned out that they brought their best lineup with mighty inferno and ono plus dahmer and daan - this evening just wasn't supposed to go easy...


chapter 1, openopholis
we had good start (quad+rl) and we made good use of that, assassins started to defend in low and it stayed this way for almost all game as we weren't able to make full lock and they weren't able to take tele/q back, our full lead from start to end, good opening for this - as it turned out - long evening


chapter 2, bright side of the castle
keyser replaced blasp at this one as he had more stable ping and a lot more of castle experiences which we were counting on :) unfortunately keyser's shape ain't these days what it used to be when he played for fs (inactivity sux :<), so he couldn't beat swedes alone and the rest of us just wasn't good enough to help him... this game started funny cause no one had spawn in rl, it was taken in 0:24 by dahmer and it confused us very much so we weren't able to time it properly for long time (always 5 secs too late and waiting for 25 ;)), we eventually stole a quad or two and managed to decrease assassins lead to 30 frags but then we rushed to much and their advantage started to increase again, it stayed this way till the very end


chapter 3, assassinated luck
if that was possible i would put good amount of money before the game started on this map as the real decider of the match... the game was very even from the start - we got quad and rl, assassins took penta and lg, dying fast our quad didn't give us any real adantage so they took the lead in the early stage of the game, but it was never more than 20 frags... avenger played this part quite smart and he eventually even managed to steal 2nd penta and my bore-rlpack, that alone let us to take 2nd breath and assassins lead was curbed to only 12 frags with 10 remaining minutes

with 3 rls and having ra/q more less secured we were quite sure to take 3rd penta and make a nice lock to get the lead but hey, surprise: http://img529.imageshack.us/img529/6486/ezquake088tj4.j pg :) rl + 2xlg ain't something you like when you trying to make penta-stealing from window... i died instantly and since only sg-blsp decided to help assassins took the 3rd penta... the only good thing out if it was that we still had two 200/100/rls wandering around...

for the first time in this game we took the lead in 13th minute and by 4th penta we were leading with 26 frags, this time we were a bit smarter and attacked penta from 2 sides at once killing enemy rls during the process (http://img118.imageshack.us/img118/4876/ezquake088aqg1.jpg) , we managed to take ra/q after that and this gave us 2 precious minutes, cause they overtook the map 3 minutes before the end while we were leading with 30 frags... valla alone makes 30 frags in a minute (eql4 vs us :E) so it was quite dangerous to start hiding so early, but we tried it and it eventually worked out somehow this time, pure luck :D


chapter 4, dark side of the castle
we forgot to call mr brightside this time and it weren't good for us, besides first even minutes no big story here, assassins locked it and didn't let us do anything, in the middle of the game we started telling jokes so the rest of the game passed in nice atmoshpere ;)


chapter 5, decideropholis
the game started well - avenger took high-rl+q and killed enemy rl, we also took next 2 rls... lock? almost... i guess at this point each of us was kinda tired because from this moment instead of locking the map (3 rls vs 0 rls) we let assassins to take low under their control - even though avenger took next two quads he couldn't get all enemies in low and they harrased us at high-rl all the time for next 4-5 minutes, moltas was so irritated that we can't get rid of that attacks that he actually whined during the game which he usually ain't doing :D, "DO OSMETHUIDN with quda (c) moltas" (http://img217.imageshack.us/img217/314/ezquake087zm7.jpg) :D

60:36 after 5 minutes - only 24 frags of advantage in 5 minutes, thats not what i call lock... in 9th minute something weird happened and we lost tele/quad, i retreated to low and the rest died (pff amateurs ;E), at this moment we had ~50 frags of advantage which isn't safe at all when it's not even half of the game but we couldn't risk the loss of last rl so we barricaded in low/ramh and successive were trying to get more rls and nip enemy quad attacks in the bud, the plan was going not bad, our advantage of ~50 frags stayed untouched for next 3-5 minutes and we were getting more rls with every minute, but lack of rox in low/ramh with 4 people around can really hurt, so eventually we all had rl with 0-3 rox :)

in the meantime we tried to jump over to tele-high/gl-mh but it wasn't easy since there always was enemy around, eventually it worked out, our efforts were rewarded, more - we even managed to take quad and kill some of enemy rls, but somehow they still managed to outkill us and take next quad... yet we stole them several precious minutes and with only 3-4 left they had to make full attack if they wanted to win the game... but that was our home map, not theirs, so we didn't let them ;)


ggs!
