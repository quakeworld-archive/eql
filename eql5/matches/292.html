They did a good comeback however and we were actually down at low the last few minutes.

DM3: This was very close considering they were _very_ lucky to get every penta. Second pent we were left with only sai at penta. I was out of ammo and ran away. They got only 3 more RAs but they had weapons when they got theirs and we only stole it with sg.

E1M2: Well fought by 3B who were in a comanding lead after 15 minutes. But we took over and the last minutes they were slowspawning with maybe a 20 frags lead. We were gaining fast with every rl on the map. The last frag for overtime would have been ours when overdose "bollade" someone with a rl shot that sent him flying but he lived. Ah so close!

DM2: Second time, again great start. This time no timeouts and no relent in our defence of tele/quad. They were never close to taking over in my opinion. We could harass them at low or keep it for a while while tele was safe. When they slipped they were without weapons. Much better TP this round.

DM3: Decider on DM3. Our worst and their best map from regular season? Well we don't care, now we are on a roll. Very good TP and help to packs this round. If we lost packs we spawned them or kept them for mates. They dissed their own rl before penta, we telefraged their rl at mega/tele and we actually got a few pentas. Everything went our way seemingly.

GGs 3B! Well played and fun games.
