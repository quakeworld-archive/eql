preliminary Divisions

2007-09-15 10:54 by sassa

Preliminary divisions have been made and we are looking for your comment before we start EQL6. You got two days to complain.

You have to consider the system that we follow with regulations.
If a team wins or is second in the division, the team has to go to the higher division next season.
However, this can be changed if we give the team a wildcard. (1-2/division)

Directly from the rules:
Bottom2 teams in the regular season will be moved down one division the following EQL if they play all matches and requests it. The only way for inactive teams to get relegated is by wildcards. Top2 teams in the regular season will be moved up one division.

When you post your complaint, include the name of your clan so we dont get 6 complaints from the same one clan.

Head over to EQL's forum section @ http://www.quakeworld.nu/forum/viewtopic.php ?pid=28297
