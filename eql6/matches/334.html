#5 2007-10-25 11:55 by ncr (130.243.208.XXX)

Jaded by their swift progress through this season's fifth division of the EQL, la! knew to be quick to ask us about our map. After a discussion that took longer than should have been necessary (sorry, la!), we decided on E1M2. This turned out to be a good choice. The first half of the map was even, with advantage shifting quickly and continuously. Then we came into it for real and seized the map. We lost control for the last few minutes though and la! was able to tidy the numbers up somewhat in their favour.

DM3 is what we would believe to be our weakest map. We have no interest whatsoever in RA. We have just recently learnt that there is a pent outside where you fetch the mega every once in a while. Consequently, we like to take it. It feels "naughty" and kind of forbidden. The game against la! on this map was extremely even. A cavalcade of bad to mediocre playing over all, but I was pleasantly surprised at how well things were going. We took control at the very end, maybe in the 18th minute or so. It was close, but their hiding techniques proved superior to our intercepting skills.

We knew they'd be good on DM2. A recent practice game came out very close, I think in our favour. We did indeed rape them on DM2 when we played them the first time, but that was during a time when they employed a bizarre habit of never topping their team -- maybe om their full potential te verstoppen. This time around, they played with a topped team, got one of the fullest starts I've ever had the pleasure to give away (I personally saw to it not to reach low RL in time at 00.01) and had practiced quite assidiously for a few months. One can only state that we should have practiced as much during season as during summer.

Also, I think we'll be more thoughtful regarding the circumstances of future EQL games. We'd been hungring for an EQL game (and pretty much for a game at all) for about three weeks when finally, AV having had no warmup and lacking a key player due to computer malfunction, and my connexion being in its shittiest, most PL-ridden state, la! after a somewhat lengthy internal discussion finally kindly agreed to play us that god-forsaken tuesday -- and thus we naggingly achieved one of our most inelegant defeats ever.

However, nothing bad that doesn't bring something good with it. I hope this defeat against la! will bring us back down to earth in some people's eyes so that they'll have the fortitude to play a few pracs against us again. It's been tiresome to be turned down by some clans when we've been looking for prac (as well as occassionally while looking for an EQL game) because they've thought us to be "too good". Thank god for CSN, eQ, Osams, CMT and the other clans in division 4. It has sometimes seemed that when you're div 5 and want someone to play, this is the place to look -- which is strange, considering the immense EQL activity in division 5 on paper.

Having said all this, one cannot escape the fact that what la! is on their way of achieving, and, regardless of the final outcome of the division, indeed what they have already achieved, is one of the most impressive rates of progression I've ever come across. They were a completely new team in the beginning of this season, and we quickly counted them out. Never would I have expected them or indeed anyone to come this far in such a short time. They have learnt a great deal to say the least, and their determination and endurance is admirable. This tuesday, it earned them the group game victory over what some people have talked of as a favourite clan in division 5 this season, and a clan that is playing its second season in the division and in the EQL in general.

I hope that now that the Game has finally been played we'll be able to play many a practice game with la!. They're a good match and as such a valuable resource. Remains to congratulate them on the victory and wish them good luck in the remaining games.

Hats off to Limbs Away! and see you in the play-offs. ;)


Note: I hope I don't come across as a whiner in the above text. The text is aimed to convey my feelings about the game and the difficulties sometimes associated with getting practice games, and while I really want to ventilate those, I would never want to point fingers, stir up drama or cause schisms.
