EQL7 Summer signups are open!

2008-04-29 21:11 by Itsinen

The summer is getting closer but we haven't forgotten our fellow quakers!

The signups are now open!

As the signups are open don't forget that you signup for a 2on2 and a 4on4 league. Remember the max of 8 players in your team, so consider and ask around who will be active and around for this season. The regular season is only 5 weeks long so your clanmates should be able to tell if they can play or not!

There will also be a teambuilding for the rookies as usually; we just hope that there will be enough teams to make it interesting for them as well.
http://www.quakeworld.nu/forum/viewtopic.php ?id=2949

For those who have not heard or seen any news about upcoming EQL I suggest reading the following link. It's shortly about the biggest changes for this season:
http://www.quakeworld.nu/news/283/

Signups closes on Saturday the 10th 18:00 CEST.

For those who are searching a clan or building one:
http://www.quakeworld.nu/forum/viewforum.php ?id=11

Be fast as there is not much time until the season begins!

And last but not least, read the rules! There are quite many changes!

Happy signups!

The admins
