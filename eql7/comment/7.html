2008-05-21 15:16 by Nas

HOT HOT HOT Div2!!!

I put together a little something about all the clans in div2, enjoy!!

:: Chosen
After shocking everyone in NQR11 by almost taking a gold medal in the silver cup its gonna be fun to see what they can accomplish in EQL summer aswell, i have high expectations for this team now. Nice to see that this clan finaly got some good teamplay going after alot of season as a bottom clan.
Best Players: Mythic, 187

:: Dies Ater
Try to beat this clan on dm2, im sorry you cant... soma is 2hot4dm2.
With a solid lineup of players wich would suit div1 i think we are gonna see this clan at the top of div2, thats if they get some good teamplay going. There has been alot of clans in the history of quakeworld with a strong lineup on paper but then lose because they lack teamplay.
Best Players: Spoink, Creature

:: Dogs
Ok.. tea and scones @ dm3? ;), Jokes aside, this is an intresting lineup of englishmen, americans and one happy little swede, i havnt yet seen this clan play any 4on4 but i think they are gonna do well in div2 this season.

:: Hippushnik
They got the bronze in hockey but what can this finnish clan do in EQL div2, alot in my oppinion, this clan realy do have great teamplay and are not easy to handle on any map, even tho in a clinch they never give up and i think they are gonna do very well this season.
Best Players: Katsku, Joulukuu

:: Mean Machine
They realy are mean machines... and now they got Moltas, div2 watch out i say. This is a clan that can work miracles with the right lineup.
Best Players: Moltas, Skillah

:: No Pasaran!
"Soviet Big 7" an expression some may heard of... this is it.
They play with big confidence and sometimes it feels like these guys just cant die. A strong russian team they can make it to the top this season.
Best Players: Gor, Zepp

:: Nordic animals in lotion
The Union of Sweden and Norway, with div1 players such as tco and grisling, can div2 handle this?. Yes, i think so. Have this clan even played a single prac?, I think it looks better than it is and the other clans without stars will beat this clan with good teamplay, individual skill is imortant but not as important as good teamplay!.
Best Players: Tco, Grisling

:: Oblivion
My own lovely clan. We have been playing alot of pracs lately and got a good teamplay going, we still miss hagge but i think we are gonna do well this season.
Best Players: Djevulsk, Eta-Beta
