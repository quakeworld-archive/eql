Deleted teams and playoff info!

2009-04-07 15:53 by

Four teams has now been deleted due to inactivity. One team in div1, one team in div2 and two teams in div3. Also important info regarding playoff!

A total of 4 teams has been removed this season due to inactivity. The clans that has been removed are the following:

Division 1: Vagabond
Division 2: AntiQuad
Division 3: Parolan Visa & TIEPS08A

Sadly this also means that the teams that have played vs any of these teams will get their points withdrawn and games has been removed. (Note that giving everyone else WO against these teams would have the exact same result in standings.)

Info about playoffs:

We are currently in week 8 of 10 in groupstages. So teams that have a lot of games left to play, play your games asap!

Playoff weeks will begin April 27th (week 11 in EQL).

Week 11: Quarter-finals
Week 12: Semi-finals
Week 13: Finals

(If teams for some reason can't play these weeks, let us know in advance!)

Top 8 teams from each division will advance to playoff in their division. And as usual the winner of each division faces #8, #2 faces #7 and so on.

Playoffs are played BO5. Regarding maps in playoffs: First all three maps are played. If the game score is 2-1 after first three maps, the team with 1 mapwin will choose the next map. Then if a 5th map is needed, it's the other teams turn to pick decidermap.

Have fun and GL!
