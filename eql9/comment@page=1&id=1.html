EQL 9 Signups and Rules!

2009-01-28 00:07 by Itsinen

Signups are up and we hope you'll enjoy this season! Also a BIG change in rules, read more by clicking on the link...

... First of all, WELCOME! We are back and ready to upload some hot games on our site again!


Signups:
Are GO! So signup your team for some 4on4 if you at least have 4 active players that are really active. Requirements of this season is that you have a squad that is capable of playing 2 official games/week. As the activity has increased really much lately we have no doubts that there wouldn't be several, if not almost all capable of doing this. So in reality this means less pracs and more officials than before.

Changes:
This season will be LONGER, BIGGER and hopefully also better. We will have TB3 as maps this season. So friends of not kenyans should be happy!
This season is also longer than the last 2 seasons, we will have ~9 weeks of regular season to play instead 6 as earlier. But there is a reason for this -> Divisions are bigger which means more games, more drama and of course more fragging! Even if the season is longer you should be able to field players more active than in the last two seasons.

Site:
Thanks to johnny_cz's KT Log Parser, fog added stats to the match pages. So make sure to upload your .log-files when you report games for some nice statistics!

Rules:
Fuhquake, Mqwcl are not listed as accepted clients anymore. Also versions before 1.9 of Ezquake have been removed. So plainly explained: Only new Ezquake and FTE clients are accepted. There will soonish be new clients (rumours) of both clients which will have new f_ruleset to check for cheats. So to make this crashlanding easier on fuh and mqwcl users we have decided to remove them already instead of midseason. Read more about it on the rules section.
Before these new versions of the clients are out we will have to use following commands before starting our games:
f_ruleset
f_modified
f_scripts
f_skins (enemyskinforcing is illegal)

Yes, we know, it's lots of writing. If these procedures are not done before game has started and other clients have been used there will be points deductions! Also notice that Mqwcl and Fuh will not answer to these commands.
Once again, read more about it at the rules section. Most people should know anyways what those values should be.
Also if somebody doesn't have these settings as they should then MAKE them change them. There are some huge advantages of having these turned on/tweaked.

Longer season:
As earlier mentioned as it is a longer season we have also increased the amount of players in a team to 10. Also additions/swaps increased from 2 to 3. It wouldn't also hurt to ask your mates if they are going abroad or similar things to prevent losses of players during mid-season.

Walkovers:
The WO-system is totally different, instead of giving points for having problems with fielding players we are mostly taking away points this season instead. So yes, you might suddenly miss 3 points after 3 weeks or so. Don't be surprised. In cases when Clan A has played against Clan C, but Clan C has not been able to field players against Clan B the admins might consider giving a "normal wo" to Clan B, IF it makes a difference that the clans haven't played. Mostly points deduction anyways.

Bunnyscripts:
..is not legal to use in EQL games. Later this season as new clients will be up for use this will easily be removed. Be FAIR until that and remove the existing ones in your config.

Hopefully you'll frag often and well!
Yours, the admins!
