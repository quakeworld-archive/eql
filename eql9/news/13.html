Bloodpunch kicked from EQL9

2009-05-12 23:49 by Zalon

The division 3 team, Bloodpunch played their Quarter-final game vs Comfortably Numb yesterday. In that match the team used a player named PICKE89.

Even before the game had started, the player was under suspicion for not being the player approved for Bloodpunch.

Read on for the full statement

After investigating in the matter, we can now confirm that the player playing as PICKE89 in yesterdays game, was indeed not the PICKE signed up for Bloodpunch.

This means that Bloodpunch will be kicked for breaking the mercing rule.
-"Playing in more than one team, or playing under someone else's name in another team (mercing)"

As the investigation have shown that only one person within the Bloodpunch team knew about the player not being PICKE, we have decided not to punish other members of the team.

The players known in the scene as Stickman and Phrenic, will each be excluded for participating in EQL until halfway through next season.

This also means that Comfortably Numb will go on to the semi-finals.
